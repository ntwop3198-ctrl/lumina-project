## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Guqin]
[Dizi]
[Spoken]
Khanka waters meet the sky, rice scent over the border lake
Waterbirds fly through the reeds, one stretch of water mirrors the dawn

[Verse 1]
By Lake Khanka the fields are wide
Birds call through the reeds
Green plains for a thousand li on both shores
One clear lake reflects the sky

[Pre-Chorus]
Mishan and Hulin lean on each other
Black earth grows good fields
Fishing and farming through wind and rain
A thousand waves stir in the heart

[Chorus]
Lake Khanka, wide border lake
Endless blue water touching the clouds
Content with stillness, hidden in the reeds
One fishing song, year after year

[Verse 2]
The great lake vast, the small lake close
Countless birds come home with the sunset
Evening boats return with distant sails
One story passed from age to age

[Pre-Chorus]
Mishan and Hulin lean on each other
Rice blossoms scent a thousand li
Working on without rest, we go far
Where the heart rests is home

[Chorus]
Lake Khanka, wide border lake
Endless blue water touching the clouds
Content with stillness, hidden in the reeds
One fishing song, year after year

[Bridge]
The lake shines in silence, meeting the sky
Standing on our own, we never bend
Bowing to work the lake, honoring the small
Wind, frost, rain and snow, swallowing the toil
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
By the reeds, life goes on

[Final Chorus]
Lake Khanka, a name that lasts forever
Silent blue water holds a thousand dreams
Tilling for ages, keeping the first heart
The wide lake light shines on forever

[Outro]
By the reeds, water and sky go on
The border lake wide, flowing long
[Fade Out]
```
