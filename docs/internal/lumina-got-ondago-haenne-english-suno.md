## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
In Pyongyang he was a doctor
War came and people went south
Just for a little while, he said
I will be back soon, he said

[Verse 2]
He left his wife and children there
He held one son by the hand
He must have looked back at the door
Not knowing it was the last time

[Chorus]
I will be back soon, he said
He said it and walked out
Forty years went by
The words are still there
He was not lying
Back then he truly thought it would be soon

[Verse 3]
Down in Busan he raised a tent
He took in those with no money
If someone could not pay to leave
They say he opened the back door at night

[Verse 4]
Someone offered to send him north
Others cannot go, he said, so neither can I
He never married again
He wrote letters he could not send

[Bridge]
One promise, back soon
He carried it forty years
In one back door he saw his work
He did not turn from nights of growing old alone
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Having nothing, he could open everything

[Chorus]
I will be back soon, he said
He said it and walked out
Forty years went by
The words are still there
He was not lying
Back then he truly thought it would be soon

[Verse 5]
He built a hospital and healed people
Yet had no house of his own
He lived out his days in a rooftop room
They say it was Christmas, before dawn

[Outro]
I will be back soon, he said
He never went
Only the words remain
He has not gone yet
[End]
```
