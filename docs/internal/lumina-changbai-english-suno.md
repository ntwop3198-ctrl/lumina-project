## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Guqin]
[Dizi]
[Spoken]
Changbai stands leaning on the clouds, Heaven Lake mirrors the cold of the snow
A sea of forest and snowfields stretch wide, one range holding a thousand years

[Verse 1]
Changbai stands guard over the northern sky
Heaven Lake mirrors the green ridges
The sacred land of Guandong for a thousand years
Pines stand tall in the snow

[Pre-Chorus]
The forest sea is vast, the snowfields wide
The warm land of Guandong guards the pass
Bones forged on ice and snow for ages
A great spirit breaks through every hardship

[Chorus]
Changbai Mountains, sacred Heaven Lake
Forest sea and snowfields touching the sky
Content in silence, keeping the forest
One lake of heaven, year after year

[Verse 2]
Heaven Lake holds the icy mist
Ancient trees rise into the sky
Hunters songs echo far in the hills
A brave wind passed from age to age

[Pre-Chorus]
The forest sea is vast, the snowfields wide
The warm land of Guandong guards the pass
Only the steadfast travel far
Standing through the frost, lighting the world

[Chorus]
Changbai Mountains, sacred Heaven Lake
Forest sea and snowfields touching the sky
Content in silence, keeping the forest
One lake of heaven, year after year

[Bridge]
Heaven Lake like a mirror, the forest without a sound
Walking the snow on our own, leaning on no one
Bowing to dig wild ginseng, honoring the small
Through bitter snow and cold, hardening the bones
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Forest sea and snowfields, life goes on

[Final Chorus]
Changbai Mountains, a name that lasts forever
The silent forest guards a thousand years
Keeping the first heart through the ages
The sacred mountain holding up the sky

[Outro]
On the Changbai heights, by Heaven Lake
Forest sea and snowfields, life goes on
[Fade Out]
```
