# 心造的重量 — ZH·EN·JA·VI Suno 붙여넣기

곡 설명 칸은 비운다. 브랜드송 해당 없음.  
브리지 6줄. 5행은 언어 시그니처. **진공묘유 없음.**  
VI 5행은 KO `구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고` (VI 시그니처 미잠금).  
JA 후렴은 `すべては心がつくる`. 一切唯心造 한자를 넣지 않는다.

## ZH · 心造的重量

```
心造的重量
```

```
Mandarin quiet contemplative ballad, low strings and still pause, mature male Mandarin vocal, metaphor of weight without diagnosis or healing promise, 72bpm, Mandarin only
```

```
upbeat, EDM, rap, trap, heavy drums, autotune, choir, bright pop, female vocal
```

```
[Intro]
[Low strings]
原以为只是一句老话
活过才知道 化成了重量

[Verse 1]
都说万事皆由心生
我只当是一句文字匆匆走过
表里不一的日子一天天堆积
我看见那重量总会留在某处

[Verse 2]
心不安 夜也难安
心沉重 脚步也沉重
藏着压着的日子越是漫长
那份担子就悄悄渗进哪里

[Pre-Chorus]
该怪的不只是风
怎样用这颗心
有时会改变我脚下的路
只是不知道那是否就是全部

[Chorus]
一切唯心造 心会造出重量
藏起的心 藏多少就有多重
安然的心 安多少就有多轻
那重量有时会留在脚步上
管好这颗心 就是管好自己

[Verse 3]
好像骗过了谁
骗得最久的却是我自己
如今我开始听一听身体的话
从脚步里读懂心留下的重量

[Bridge]
我还想再握住什么
放下一些 问问自己
不再怪谁 回头看看自己
空出来的地方 呼吸慢慢平稳
求小求苦求己, 不向外求
心若轻了 脚步也轻

[Chorus]
一切唯心造 心会造出重量
藏起的心 藏多少就有多重
安然的心 安多少就有多轻
那重量有时会留在脚步上
管好这颗心 就是管好自己

[Outro]
[Lingering strings]
看脚步就看见心
看心就看见走过的地方
```

## EN · The Weight the Mind Makes

```
The Weight the Mind Makes

```

```

```
upbeat, EDM, rap, trap, heavy drums, autotune, choir, bright pop, female vocal
```

```
[Intro]
[Low strings]
I thought it was just an old saying
But living on, it stayed as weight

[Verse 1]
They say that everything begins in the mind
I passed it by as just a line of words
But when the days of a two-faced heart pile up
I have seen that weight settle somewhere

[Verse 2]
When the heart is not at ease, the night is not at ease
When the heart is heavy, so are my steps
The longer I hide and hold things down
The deeper that burden seeps somewhere

[Pre-Chorus]
It is not only the wind to blame
How I chose to use my heart
Can change the road beneath my feet
Though I do not know if that is all

[Chorus]
All is made by the mind, the mind makes its weight
A hidden heart weighs as much as it hides
An easy heart is as light as it rests
And that weight can linger in my steps
To tend my heart is to tend myself

[Verse 3]
It felt like I was fooling someone
But the one I fooled the longest was me
Now I listen a little to what my body says
I read the weight of my heart in how I walk

[Bridge]
What was I still trying to hold
I let some go and ask myself
I set down blame and look within
In the emptied space my breath grows calm
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
When the heart is light, the steps are light

[Chorus]
All is made by the mind, the mind makes its weight
A hidden heart weighs as much as it hides
An easy heart is as light as it rests
And that weight can linger in my steps
To tend my heart is to tend myself

[Outro]
[Lingering strings]

生きてみたら重さとして残った

[Verse 1]

[Bridge]
まだ何をつかもうとしていたのか
少し手放して自分に問いかける
人を責めずに自分を見つめる
空いた場所で息が少し整う
小を求め 苦を求め 己を求め, 外に求めず
心が軽ければ足取りも軽い

[Chorus]
すべては心がつくる 心がつくる重さがある
隠した心は隠したぶん重く
安らかな心は安らかなぶん軽い
その重さが足取りに残ることもある

[Lingering strings]
