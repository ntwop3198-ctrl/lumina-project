# 法如泡沫 — ZH·EN·JA·VI Suno 붙여넣기

곡 설명 칸은 비운다. 브랜드송 해당 없음.

## ZH · 法如泡沫

```
法如泡沫
```

```
Mandarin Chinese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, serene closing with a sense of full circle, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Mandarin vocal, 70 BPM, Mandarin Chinese only
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
无量世界装满珍宝布施
不如受持此经四句 为人说的福大
如何为人演说
不取于相 如如不动

[Verse 2]
一切有为法
如梦 如幻
如泡 如影
如露 亦如电

[Pre-Chorus]
应作
如是观

[Chorus]
法如泡沫
浮起又消 凝结又干
一闪而来 一闪而去
没有一样可以抓
法如泡沫
所以活得轻

[Verse 3]
[Warm and luminous, gentle acoustic strum]
佛说完了经
须菩提 比丘 比丘尼
天上人间 皆大欢喜
信受奉行 各回各路

[Pre-Chorus]
应作
如是观

[Chorus]
法如泡沫
浮起又消 凝结又干
一闪而来 一闪而去
没有一样可以抓
法如泡沫
所以活得轻

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
从梦里醒来 是早晨
洗了脚 铺好座 坐下
应作
진공묘유
求小求苦求己, 不向外求
所以活得轻

[Chorus]
[Very calm and fading]
法如泡沫
浮起又消 凝结又干
一闪而来 一闪而去
没有一样可以抓
法如泡沫
所以活得轻

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak into silence]
一切有为法 如梦幻泡影
如露亦如电 应作如是观
```

## EN · The Dharma Is Like a Bubble

```
The Dharma Is Like a Bubble
```

```
English acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, serene closing with a sense of full circle, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male English vocal, 70 BPM, English only
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Fill measureless worlds with treasure and give it all
yet holding four lines of this and telling them is greater
and how should it be told
not grasping at forms, unmoved and still

[Verse 2]
All things that are made
are like a dream, like an illusion
like a bubble, like a shadow
like the dew, like lightning

[Pre-Chorus]
See them
just like this

[Chorus]
The Dharma is like a bubble
rising and gone, gathering and dry
a flash it comes, a flash it goes
nothing there to hold
The Dharma is like a bubble
and so we live lightly

[Verse 3]
[Warm and luminous, gentle acoustic strum]
When the Buddha's words were finished
Subhuti, the monks and the nuns
heaven and earth were filled with joy
they trusted, took it in, and went their way

[Pre-Chorus]
See them
just like this

[Chorus]
The Dharma is like a bubble
rising and gone, gathering and dry
a flash it comes, a flash it goes
nothing there to hold
The Dharma is like a bubble
and so we live lightly

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
I wake from the dream to morning
I wash my feet, spread my seat and sit
See them
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
and so we live lightly

[Chorus]
[Very calm and fading]
The Dharma is like a bubble
rising and gone, gathering and dry
a flash it comes, a flash it goes
nothing there to hold
The Dharma is like a bubble
and so we live lightly

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak into silence]
All conditioned things are like a dream, an illusion, a bubble, a shadow
like dew, like lightning, see them in this way
```

```

[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
量り知れない世界を宝で満たして施しても

[Verse 2]

夢のよう 幻のよう

露のよう 稲妻のよう

見なさい

[Chorus]
法は泡のごとし

法は泡のごとし

須菩提も比丘も比丘尼も

[Pre-Chorus]
このように
見なさい

[Chorus]
法は泡のごとし
浮かんでは消え 結んでは乾き
ぴかりと来て ぴかりと去る
つかむものはひとつもない
法は泡のごとし

[Guitar drops, daegeum only]

足を洗い 座を敷いて座った
```
