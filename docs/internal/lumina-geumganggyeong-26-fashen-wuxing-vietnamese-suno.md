Vietnamese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, calm and deep with a touch of warmth, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Vietnamese vocal, 70 BPM, Vietnamese only
