## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Acoustic guitar only]

[Verse 1]
That man who always had a way with words
Is quiet tonight
He just keeps turning his glass
And staring out the window

[Verse 2]
I asked him what was wrong
He only said the kid's… not doing so well lately
He said that much
And didn't say another word

[Chorus]
A man who'll talk about the world all night
Gets stuck when it comes to his own child
It's not that he has nothing to say
It's that words won't fix it
So he keeps his mouth shut

[Verse 3]
I swallowed the know-it-all words
The line that I've been there too
Because that kind of thing isn't comfort
I just poured him one more glass

[Bridge]
That's what being a parent is
Out in the world you know it all
But once you're back inside your home
There's nothing you know how to do
I know that feeling
So I didn't add a word

[Last Chorus]
A man who'll talk about the world all night
Tonight just empties his glass
On days the words won't come
You just sit there by his side

[Outro]
I folded up my goodbye too
Just a pat on the shoulder
And I turned away
[Guitar fade out]
```
