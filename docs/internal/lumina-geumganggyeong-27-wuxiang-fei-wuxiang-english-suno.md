## Title

```
Formless Is Not Nothing
```

부제: Diamond Sutra Chapter 27, Neither Cut Off Nor Extinguished  
문서 한글: **무상은 무상이 아니다** · 보컬 **남성** · 따뜻하고 차분하게

English acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, quiet yet quietly hopeful like winter turning to spring, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male English vocal, 70 BPM, English only

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Because I said form is not form
do not think the Tathagata awoke with no form
do not hold such a thought
that too is just another form

[Verse 2]
Because I said there is no form
do not say that all is cut off and gone
one who sets the heart on awakening
makes no form of cut off and gone either

[Pre-Chorus]
Being empty
is not being nothing

[Chorus]
Formless is not nothing
do not dwell on the thought of nothing
because it's empty, it can hold
where it was cut, it carries on
Formless is not nothing
in the empty place a flower blooms

[Verse 3]
[Soft picking, subtle warmth bloom]
A winter tree with every leaf let go
I thought that it had died
then spring came and on every branch
new buds had broken through

[Pre-Chorus]
Being empty
is not being nothing

[Chorus]
Formless is not nothing
do not dwell on the thought of nothing
because it's empty, it can hold
where it was cut, it carries on
Formless is not nothing
in the empty place a flower blooms

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
spring arrives at the bare branch tips
being empty, all comes into bloom
Being empty
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
in the empty place a flower blooms

[Chorus]
Formless is not nothing
do not dwell on the thought of nothing
because it's empty, it can hold
where it was cut, it carries on
Formless is not nothing
in the empty place a flower blooms

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
One who sets the heart on supreme awakening
does not speak of dharmas as cut off and gone
```
