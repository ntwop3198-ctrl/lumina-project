## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
A little temple at the far mountain's foot
An old monk who never lay down all his life
A few pieces of fruit for his offering
He sat upright and stayed awake through the night

[Verse 2]
On the day he left, he said just one thing
Nothing came and nothing went
So nothing happened at all
He smiled softly and closed his eyes

[Pre-Chorus]
Just brushed it off
And lightly went

[Chorus]
Those who brushed it off and went
Holding nothing in their hands
Came like the wind
And went like the wind
Laughing light and free
Those who brushed it off and went

[Interlude]

[Verse 3]
A cigarette at the dinner table
The old scholar who explained the Book of Changes and the sutras
Though followers came in crowds
He laughed and said, I have no disciples

[Verse 4]
The great monk who built hundreds of temples
Never kept a single room for himself
Not a roof tile, not a handful of earth
He waved his hand and said none of it was his

[Pre-Chorus]
Disciples and believers
He called them all fellow travelers

[Chorus]
Those who brushed it off and went
Holding nothing in their hands
Came like the wind
And went like the wind
Laughing light and free
Those who brushed it off and went

[Bridge]
Send no notice, just sing a song for me, he said
A few fruits, one smoke, empty hands, a road that still felt full
Not running from pain or age, meeting them with a smile
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
진공묘유, the bright and open sky
When my day comes, I'll go standing, with a smile

[Final Chorus]
Those who brushed it off and went
Holding nothing in their hands
Came like the wind
And went like the wind
Laughing light and free
Those who brushed it off and went

[Outro]
Those who brushed it off
And went
Like the wind
```
