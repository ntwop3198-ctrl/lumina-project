## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Subhuti, what do you think
Can the Tathagata be seen by the form of the body
No, World Honored One
He cannot be seen by the form of the body

[Verse 2]
For the bodily form the Tathagata speaks of
Is not a bodily form
The Buddha gave a nod
And laid one more word upon it

[Pre-Chorus]
All that has form
Is empty and false

[Chorus]
Form is not form
Do not trust only what you see
Know that form is not form
And right there you will see the Tathagata
Form is not form
Right there the Tathagata is

[Verse 3]
Last night a cloud was a rabbit
Then a dragon, then it hid the moon
Even my face in the mirror
Is someone new each year

[Pre-Chorus]
All that has form
Is empty and false

[Chorus]
Form is not form
Do not trust only what you see
Know that form is not form
And right there you will see the Tathagata
Form is not form
Right there the Tathagata is

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
Clouds may scatter, the sky stays the same
Beyond all form, one round moon
All that has form
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
Right there the Tathagata is

[Chorus]
Form is not form
Do not trust only what you see
Know that form is not form
And right there you will see the Tathagata
Form is not form
Right there the Tathagata is

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
All that has form is empty and false
See all forms as no form, and you see the Tathagata
```
