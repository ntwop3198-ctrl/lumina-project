## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
Even with nothing held in my hands
Kicking up dust until the sun went down
Back when I raced along the alleyways
A single laugh could fill the whole wide world

[Verse 2]
I didn't know how to weigh the gain and loss
I didn't know how to measure me against the rest
If I had run my heart out through the day
That alone was more than plenty

[Pre-Chorus]
Ever since I pinned on a grown-up's name tag
Why has the world become so tangled and so heavy
I fill and fill, yet my heart keeps running empty
The more I have, why do I feel poorer still

[Chorus]
That simple heart, those crystal-clear days
Days that shone so bright, though we had so little
Looking back I know the road I'm meant to walk
With that clear heart, once again I'll go

[Verse 3]
Why does the moon keep following me
Where does the red sun go to hide, I used to ask
All those innocent question marks
I let them fade in the rush of busy years

[Bridge]
구소구고구기, I lay down every tangled thought
When I gently ease the weight of wanting
At last the place of 진공묘유 begins to glow
Emptied out and clear, simplicity is peace

[Chorus]
That simple heart, those crystal-clear days
Days that shone so bright, though we had so little
Looking back I know the road I'm meant to walk
With that clear heart, once again I'll go

[Outro]
Just breathing light and clear
On the road back to who I really am
I'll live holding that one simple heart
```
