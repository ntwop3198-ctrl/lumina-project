## Style

```
Chinese acoustic folk blues shuffle with subtle erhu color in the intro and outro, 96 BPM loose shuffle, raspy male baritone talk-singing with a grin, loose strummed acoustic guitar and answering slide guitar, walking upright bass, brush snare, harmonica fills, hand claps and backing hums in the chorus, guitar-only bridge with quiet spoken voice, warm tender final chorus, erhu and one guitar note fading out, Mandarin vocals, short Korean phrases in chorus, short Korean chant in bridge
```
