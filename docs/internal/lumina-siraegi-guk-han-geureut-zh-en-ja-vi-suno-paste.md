## ZH · 一碗萝卜缨汤

```
一碗萝卜缨汤
```

```
Acoustic folk ballad, close-miked fingerpicked guitar with intimate string detail, soft piano, distant harmonica and light erhu accents, gentle storytelling male vocal, unhurried sway, warm natural mix, Mandarin Chinese only
```

```
EDM, trap, hip hop, autotune, heavy metal, rock, dramatic belting, female vocal
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft fingerpicked guitar, distant harmonica]

[Verse 1]
小时候的晚饭桌上天天是那碗汤
萝卜缨漂在大酱汤里满满一碗
羡慕别人家桌上有肉吃
拿着勺子挑来挑去耍脾气

[Verse 2]
房梁上挂满一串串酱块 味道冲鼻
屋檐下的萝卜缨在风里慢慢晾干
妈妈蹲在灶前
白白的热气里叫我多吃一点

[Chorus]
一碗萝卜缨汤 那时真不懂
吃腻的味道会这样让人想
这家饭馆那家饭馆都找遍了
能吃是能吃 可不是那个味

[Verse 3]
现在的大酱又甜又温和
却没有那种暖到心底的味道
稻草里发酵 缸里慢慢存
等上一两年的味道去了哪里

[Bridge]
一样的砂锅 一样地熬
就那双手的味道学不来
腌酱的手 生火的那双手
那双手放进去的不只是大酱

[Final Chorus]
[Soft strings enter]
一碗萝卜缨汤 现在才明白
那一勺里装的是什么
再也吃不到的妈妈的味道
今天又舀一勺 想起那间厨房

[Outro]
小时候那么讨厌的那碗汤
如今成了最想吃的一桌饭
[Harmonica fades out]
```

## EN · A Bowl of Dried Radish Greens Soup

```
A Bowl of Dried Radish Greens Soup
```

```
Acoustic folk ballad, close-miked fingerpicked guitar with intimate string detail, soft piano, distant harmonica and light erhu accents, gentle storytelling male vocal, unhurried sway, warm natural mix, English only
```

```
EDM, trap, hip hop, autotune, heavy metal, rock, dramatic belting, female vocal
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft fingerpicked guitar, distant harmonica]

[Verse 1]
Every supper when I was small that same soup
Dried radish greens floating in a bowl of soybean paste
I envied the houses with meat on the table
Poking at my spoon I would sulk and complain

[Verse 2]
Soybean blocks hung from the rafters smelling strong
Under the eaves the greens swayed drying in the wind
My mother crouched before the fire
In the white steam telling me eat up eat more

[Chorus]
A bowl of dried radish greens soup I never knew back then
That taste I was so tired of I would miss like this
I have tried this place and that place all over town
It is good enough but it is not that taste

[Verse 3]
Today's soybean paste is sweeter and mild
But it lacks the taste that eased me deep inside
Fermented in rice straw and aged in clay jars
That taste of a year or two of waiting where did it go

[Bridge]
Same clay pot boiled the very same way
But the touch of her hands no one can copy
Hands that made the paste hands that lit the fire
What those hands put in was more than soybean paste

[Final Chorus]
[Soft strings enter]
A bowl of dried radish greens soup now I finally know
What was held in every single spoonful
My mother's taste I will never have again
Today I lift a spoon and see that kitchen once more

[Outro]
The soup I hated so much as a child
Is now the meal I miss the most
[Harmonica fades out]
```

干し菜の浮かんだテンジャン汁一杯
肉のおかずの家がただうらやましくて
スプーンをいじってだだをこねた

[Verse 2]
天井にはメジュがずらり 鼻をつく匂い
軒下には干し菜が風に揺れて乾く
かまどの前にしゃがんだ母さん
真っ白な湯気の中でたくさん食べなと言った

[Verse 3]

腹の底までほどいてくれたあの味がない
わらで寝かせて甕で熟成させて

同じ土鍋で同じように煮ても

[Final Chorus]

[Outro]
幼い頃あんなに嫌いだったあの汁が
今はいちばん食べたい食卓になった

[Intro]
