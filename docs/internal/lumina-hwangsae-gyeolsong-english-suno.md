## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Janggu]
[Verse 1]
A rich man in Gyeongsang, a distant kin came by
Same ancestors, so why are you the only one rich
Hand over half your fortune, he demanded
The rich man, wronged, went to the court of justice
[Verse 2]
Trusting he was right, he waited quietly
But the other side greased the officials behind the scenes
In the end the court said split the fortune in half
The rich man felt the sky fall on him
[Chorus]
So the rich man asked to tell one story
Hear me, officials, there was once such a trial
The singing contest the stork once judged
How much it looks like this trial today
[Verse 3]
An oriole, a cuckoo and an ibis gathered to argue
Whose voice was the sweetest and finest
They could not decide, so they left it to the stork
The ibis had already brought him a frog
[Chorus]
So the rich man asked to tell one story
Hear me, officials, there was once such a trial
The singing contest the stork once judged
How much it looks like this trial today
[Bridge]
The oriole too sorrowful, no use, he said
The cuckoo too shabby, out it goes
One frog tipped the scales
The wronged songs hung their heads
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
He judged the ibis rough cry the best
[Verse 4]
Hearing the story, the officials turned red
Not a word, they only hung their heads
What did the stork beak point to
Was it not the face of a judge who took a bribe
[Final Chorus]
So the rich man left one story behind
Hear me, later folks, there was once such a trial
The singing contest the stork once judged
Somewhere, even now, it plays out again
[Outro]
The janggu fades and the stage grows dark
Oriole, cuckoo and ibis all are gone
Only one thing remains, eyes that know shame
Without even that, no stork can help
[Janggu]
[Fade Out]

```
