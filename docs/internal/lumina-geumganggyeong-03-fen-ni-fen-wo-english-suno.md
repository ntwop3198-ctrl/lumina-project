## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Born of eggs, born of wombs
Born of moisture, born of change
With form or without form
I will carry them all across, not one left behind

[Verse 2]
Countless beings carried across
Yet not a single one was carried
Is that not strange, I asked
That is a bodhisattva, He said

[Pre-Chorus]
The moment I say I saved them
I have already drifted away

[Chorus]
Say me, say you, and you are no bodhisattva
The thought of self, the thought of other
The thought of beings, the thought of a lifespan
The moment you draw that one line
The bodhisattva is not there
Say me, say you, and you are no bodhisattva

[Verse 3]
My mother cooked rice all her life
Never once said I fed you
And I who ate it did not know then
That she was a bodhisattva

[Pre-Chorus]

I have already drifted away

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
No hand that gave, no hand that took
```
