## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
If one filled the whole world with treasure
And gave it away, would the merit be great
Very great, World Honored One
Because that merit has no nature of merit, it is called great

[Verse 2]
But if one holds even four lines of this sutra
And explains them to others
That merit is greater than all that treasure
All Buddhas came forth from this sutra

[Pre-Chorus]
And then the Buddha
Turned it over once again

[Chorus]
The Dharma is not the Dharma
The moment you grab it and say this is the Dharma
The Dharma is not there
The Dharma is not the Dharma
Do not get caught on a name
Where you let go, there is the Dharma

[Verse 3]
Read a restaurant menu all you like
It will never fill you up
The hot soup is in the clay bowl
Not in the letters
```
