## Style

```
Chinese folk comedy song, bouncy 112 BPM kuaiban pulse, male vocal with comic storytelling pauses, bright erhu lead licks over plucky pipa, woodblock, small gong accents and buoyant bass, kuaiban-pipa interlude with offbeat gong hits, short gong slapback, tea cup clink before the fade, Vietnamese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

---

## Exclude Styles

```
heavy metal, sad ballad, dark, autotune, screaming
```

고급 설정: 기이함 **30%** · 스타일 영향 **70%** · 다양성 보통 · 개인화 끔
