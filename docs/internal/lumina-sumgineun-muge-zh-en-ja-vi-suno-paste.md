## ZH · 隐藏的重量

```
隐藏的重量
```

```
Mandarin restrained life-wisdom ballad, low cello and quiet pause, mature male Mandarin vocal, weight without sermon or collapse spectacle, 74bpm, Mandarin only
```

```
upbeat, EDM, rap, trap, heavy drums, autotune, choir, dramatic orchestral climax, female vocal
```

```
[Intro]
[Low cello]
以为藏起来就会消失

[Verse 1]
在别人面前摆出无瑕的脸
装作干净地活过一些日子
心里明明有别的东西在翻滚
表面却若无其事地笑着

[Verse 2]
藏起来 火也不会熄灭
担子总是叠成两层
一层是那颗心本身的重量
一层是怕被看穿 紧紧抓住的重量

[Pre-Chorus]
表里分开得太久
那份重量身体不会装作不知道
发凉的关节 迟缓的呼吸
藏起的岁月有时会留下痕迹

[Chorus]
欲望不是用来躲藏的墙
正面相对 反而会变轻
那些全用在掩藏上的力气
若是用来调伏自己
是隐藏的重量压住了我

[Verse 3]
好像骗过了谁
骗得最久的却是我自己
嘴巴可以一直沉默
气息却藏不住长久的谎

[Bridge]
我究竟想藏起什么
放下藏着的 问问自己
不看别人的眼 回头看自己
空出来的地方 呼吸慢慢舒展
求小求苦求己, 不向外求
用变轻的脚步再往前走

[Chorus]
欲望不是用来躲藏的墙
正面相对 反而会变轻
那些全用在掩藏上的力气
若是用来调伏自己
是隐藏的重量压住了我

[Outro]
[Lingering cello]
那想要藏起的影子
让我变得最沉重
```

## EN · The Weight of Hiding

```
The Weight of Hiding
```

```
English restrained life-wisdom ballad, low cello and quiet pause, mature male English vocal, weight without sermon or collapse spectacle, 74bpm, English only
```

```
upbeat, EDM, rap, trap, heavy drums, autotune, choir, dramatic orchestral climax, female vocal
```

```
[Intro]
[Low cello]
I thought what's hidden would disappear

[Verse 1]
In front of others with a flawless face
I lived some days pretending to be clean
While something else was boiling deep inside
On the surface I just smiled like nothing's wrong

[Verse 2]
Hiding it never puts the fire out
The burden always piles up two layers deep
One is the weight of that desire itself
One is the weight of holding on so no one sees

[Pre-Chorus]
When the outside and inside split too long
The body won't pretend it doesn't know
Aching joints, a slower breath
The hidden years can leave their marks

[Chorus]
Desire was not a wall to hide behind
Facing it, I would have grown lighter
All the strength I spent on hiding
If I had spent it taming it
The weight of hiding pressed me down

[Verse 3]
It felt like I was fooling someone
But the one I fooled the longest was me
My mouth can stay silent for a while
But my breath can't hide a lie for long

[Bridge]
What was I trying so hard to hide
I set down what I hid and ask myself
Not others' eyes, I look within
In the emptied space my breath opens up
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
With lighter steps I walk again

[Chorus]
Desire was not a wall to hide behind
Facing it, I would have grown lighter
All the strength I spent on hiding
If I had spent it taming it
The weight of hiding pressed me down

[Outro]
[Lingering cello]
The shadow I tried to hide
Was what weighed on me the most
```
