```
只凭一份信
```

## EN · On Faith Alone

```
On Faith Alone
```

```
English acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, humble and steady, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male English vocal, 70 BPM, English only
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
At dawn as many as the Ganges sands
at noon and evening just as many
through ages beyond counting
giving with their whole body

[Verse 2]
Yet one who hears this and believes
not turning away, has greater blessing
how much more who copies, reads, recites
and explains it to another

[Pre-Chorus]
Words spoken to those
who board the great vehicle

[Chorus]
On faith alone
with one heart that does not turn away
greater than all the giving morning, noon and night
a greater blessing waits right there
On faith alone
that place becomes a stupa

[Verse 3]
An old country grandfather who could not read
laid the sutra open
with rough fingers in place of letters
traced it line by line

[Pre-Chorus]
Words spoken to those
who board the great vehicle

[Chorus]
On faith alone
with one heart that does not turn away
greater than all the giving morning, noon and night
a greater blessing waits right there
On faith alone
that place becomes a stupa

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
while the mind keeps measuring, the river flows on
one nod and the far shore
Words spoken to those
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
that place becomes a stupa

[Chorus]
On faith alone
with one heart that does not turn away
greater than all the giving morning, noon and night
a greater blessing waits right there
On faith alone
that place becomes a stupa

[Outro]

know that this place is a stupa
```
