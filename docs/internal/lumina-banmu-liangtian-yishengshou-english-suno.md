## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro: bamboo flute over wind, then erhu]

[Verse 1: Male Vocal]
That year I said come away with me
And you really followed where I went
Here by this thin half acre of land
You only asked where the water was

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 2: Female Vocal]
I didn't ask for gold or a fine house
Only who would cook the porridge tomorrow
You said that life would get better
And I believed it thirty autumns long

[Pre-Chorus: Call and Response]
[Male Vocal] Furrow by furrow I turned the spring soil
[Female Vocal] Stitch by stitch I mended autumn clothes

[Chorus: Duet Harmony]
Half an acre of good land kept a lifetime
Kept till the rice grew green then gold
Kept till the children grew taller than the wall
Kept till the frost fell on our temples
Half an acre of good land kept a lifetime
It's not that we never dreamed of far away
It's just that in the soil of this field
Lie buried all our yesterdays

[Verse 3: Male Vocal]
I never dressed you in fine clothes
That's the debt I've carried all my life

[Verse 3: Female Vocal]
I've counted the calluses on your hands
Those were my dowry all along

[Bridge: instruments drop to flute and two voices]
[Female Vocal] If there's a next life will you come again
[Male Vocal] I'll come again and walk this same road
[Duet Harmony] Still this half acre of land
Still this pair of hands

[Final Chorus: strings enter, fuller]
Half an acre of good land kept a lifetime
Kept till the rice grew green then gold
Kept till the children grew taller than the wall
Kept till the frost fell on our temples
Half an acre of good land kept a lifetime
It's not that we never dreamed of far away
It's just that in the soil of this field
Lie buried all our yesterdays

[Outro: erhu alone, evening fade]
[Male Vocal] It's getting dark
[Female Vocal] Let's go home
[Duet Harmony] There's more work tomorrow
```
