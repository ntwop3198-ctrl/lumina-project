## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
When the school bell rang I ran all the way
To the creaky comic shop at the alley end
A few small coins in my pocket
I sat there till the sun went down

[Verse 2]
A pot of ramen in front of me
Lost inside a worn-out comic book
Side by side on the old corner sofa
Giggling with my friends all day

[Pre-Chorus]
Years went by, the alley was gone
That door closed without a trace
But in faded memory our secret hideout
Still shines clear inside my heart

[Chorus]
The comic shop alley, the comic shop alley
Our hideout where we rested and dreamed
Turning pages worn by our hands
Those pure and gentle days
The comic shop alley, the comic shop alley
Where the breath of my childhood stays

[Verse 3]
Now a screen in my hand shows the whole world
Whatever I want, I can find
But that yellowed paper smell, that racing page
No new screen can ever hold

[Bridge]
Looking back at people come and gone
Only scattered traces in the wind
A few small coins were all I needed
At the vanished alley, I do not look away
```
