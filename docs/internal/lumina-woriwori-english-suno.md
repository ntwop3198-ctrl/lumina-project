## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
Back then dogs had just one name
This house Wori, that house Wori
You call and never know who comes
Whoever came was our dog

[Verse 2]
No leash and no dog bowl
One lump of leftover rice was plenty
Slept in the yard, grew in the yard
Barking at night was the only job

[Pre-Chorus]
When the puppies came along
We called them oyoyoyo
Only then our voices softened
Only then we gave a little love

[Chorus]
Wori wori, wori wori
Every dog in town was Wori
Wori wori, wori wori
Whoever came was our dog
No name and still grew fine
Oyoyoyo, wori wori

[Verse 3]
Now every dog has a name
They wear clothes and even shoes
Carried around, rolled in strollers
But they still come when you call

[Bridge]
No name, but it knew its home
One dog in an empty yard was enough
Wagging its tail for leftover rice
Guarding the yard on cold nights
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Whoever came when called was our dog

[Chorus]
Wori wori, wori wori
Every dog in town was Wori
Wori wori, wori wori
Whoever came was our dog
No name and still grew fine
Oyoyoyo, wori wori

[Outro]
Even now, somewhere, if you call
One will come wagging its tail
Wori wori
```
