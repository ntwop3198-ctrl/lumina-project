## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Instrumental Intro]
[Finger Snaps]
[Walking Bass]
[Intro]
One clear day at a lakeside golf course
Three men stood side by side at the tee
Jesus and Moses, and one old man
A rather special round began
[Verse 1]
First up, Jesus, a mighty swing
The white ball splashed into the lake
He watched a moment, then walked on the water
Stood on the ripples, nice shot to the green
[Verse 2]
Next up, Moses, a big clean swing
His ball too went splash in the same lake
He raised his hand at the shore, the waters parted
Found the ball on the bottom, nice shot to the green
[Pre-Chorus]
After two amazing shots were done
The old man took the driver last
A quiet swing, the ball flew high
And splashed in the middle of the same lake
[Chorus]
A big fish in the water snapped up the ball
An eagle in the sky snatched the fish
Right as it passed over the green
The ball dropped down and rolled along
Into the cup, a hole in one, hard to believe
[Verse 3]
Jesus and Moses stared without a word
The old man calmly fixed his cap
Wind and fish and eagle all as one
Is this golf or just a circus
[Bridge]
A moment of silence on the green
Jesus walked up beside the old man
Before one little ball
Swallowed a sigh into a laugh and shook his head
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
And finally called out who he was
[Outro]
[Stop]
[Spoken]

[Rimshot]
[Fade Out]
```
