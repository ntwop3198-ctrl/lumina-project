## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Male]
Ahem, neighbors, listen how Old Kim down the village lives
Eolsigu
[Verse 1]
[Male]
Warm spring winds are blowing but his old field grows only weeds
Not one turn of the plow, he snores away napping on the porch
Yet every time the sun comes up he folds his hands and stares at the sky
Oh dear heaven, please, this year give me a great big harvest
[Verse 2]
[Male]
Boils a fat pig head and holds a grand offering
Visits a famous fortune teller for a lucky day
Ties a strip of five color cloth to a post at the edge of the field
Waits for the cold autumn wind to fill his barn to overflowing
[Pre-Chorus]
[Male]
Hey there Old Kim, did you sow before you pray
Will heaven give persimmons to bare ground, dried persimmons even
[Chorus]
[Male]
Sow no seed and pray for harvest
Shed no sweat and pray for luck
Heaven laughs in disbelief, the yellow earth laughs too
Even passing little birds hold their bellies, giggling
Sow no seed and pray for harvest
In this world you reap just what you sow
Eolssu
[Interlude]
[Verse 3]
[Male]
Look around, these days there are Old Kims everywhere
Never open a book, just stick up lucky candy for the exam
Eat pork feet till full, lie down and pray the belly shrinks
Hold one cheap lottery ticket, dreaming of overnight riches
[Pre-Chorus]
[Male]
Hey there modern Old Kims, did you plant before you wait
In a yard where nothing is planted, will a golden calf spring up
[Chorus]
[Male]
Sow no seed and pray for harvest
Shed no sweat and pray for luck
Heaven laughs in disbelief, the yellow earth laughs too
Even passing little birds hold their bellies, giggling
Sow no seed and pray for harvest
In this world you reap just what you sow
[Bridge]
[Male]
Plant beans and beans will grow, plant red beans and red beans come
Where nothing is planted, only burrs and weeds grow wild
Heaven may send the rain, the earth may open its wide arms
But digging soil and burying a seed is work for my own hands
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Sweat sown today fills the autumn barn
[Final Chorus]
[Male]
Sow no seed and pray for harvest
Shed no sweat and pray for luck
Heaven laughs out loud, the whole village laughs too
Across the land the laughter spreads
Sow no seed and pray for harvest
Reaping what your sweat has earned is a true life
Jihwaja, good
[Outro]
[Male]
Oh my, hearing the talk, Old Kim must have blushed
This spring at dawn he shouldered his plow and went out to the field
Ha ha ha, good thinking, Old Kim
[Fade Out]
```
