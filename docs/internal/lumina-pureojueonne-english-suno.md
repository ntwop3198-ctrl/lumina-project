## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
It was the country his father built
His father left too many in-laws behind
His brothers took the seat, not for long
And then it came to him

[Verse 2]
Seven years he did nothing
He only sat still and watched
Who kept how many men
Under whom stood how many, he counted

[Chorus]
He set them free
Set free the people of other houses
Those once free, he said, would be sent back
That is what he said
Those houses lost that many hands
And what they lost, his side gained

[Verse 3]
He began to choose by writing
He did not ask who the father was
He looked only at what was written
In seats kept by family, strangers sat

[Bridge]
What his father had tied up
The son untied, one by one
Seven years he counted every small number
He did not run from the seat that stood alone
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Into the emptied seats came those with no name

[Chorus]
He set them free
Set free the people of other houses
Those once free, he said, would be sent back
That is what he said
Those houses lost that many hands
And what they lost, his side gained

[Verse 4]
Later he took down many men
The founding lords vanished one by one
They say he grew wary even of his own son
In his last years no one stood by him

[Outro]
Those set free went on living
And as many faded away
Which way to write it down
Was different for each who wrote
[End]
```
