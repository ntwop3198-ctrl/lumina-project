## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
At twenty-eight he fell from his horse and broke his leg
They say he bound it with willow and rode again
He failed that year and passed at thirty-two
Even then he stayed low for a long time

[Verse 2]
He would not bend to those above, pushed down again and again
Even pushed down, he kept on mending ships
He knew they would come by sea
Till they came, he was getting ready

[Chorus]
He wrote it down and went out
How many ships came that day, he wrote
Which way the wind blew, he wrote
That his mother was unwell, he wrote that too
Then he went out
He went out and won

[Verse 3]
He was taken and beaten
He came back down in plain white
When he came there were twelve ships
With those twelve he went out

[Bridge]
He lost every post he had
And went out with twelve ships
He wrote down even a single wind
He did not spare the body that still hurt

[Chorus]

[Verse 4]
His mother faded on her way to him
He heard his son had faded in the fight
That day in his diary he wrote
He wept aloud

[Outro]
In the last battle he was struck
The fighting was not over yet
They say he said, let no one know
He left with that day unwritten
```
