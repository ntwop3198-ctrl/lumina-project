## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]
A young commander came to ask for grain
Two granaries stood behind his home
Without a pause he pointed to one
And told him to take the whole of it

A heart that gave away half of all he had
Made the visitor a friend for life
That man would later guard the east
The world long remembered him as Zhou Yu

[Verse 2]
Face to face with a young lord on one couch
He laid out the shape of the age first
He said the Han could hardly rise again
And Cao Cao could not be swept away in a day
So stand in the east like a tripod leg
And take the Yangtze to its farthest end
That vision, earlier than Longzhong
Lit the road the east would walk

[Chorus]
The open heart that shared his granary
Grew into a great design for the realm
People laughed and called him simple
Yet he saw farther than them all
He bound the alliance, took up command
He held up the realm in silence, Lu Su

[Verse 3]
When the Yangtze shook he went to Liu Bei
Reached out first and drew up the alliance
But later tales gave his credit
Quietly to another wisdom

When Zhou Yu had left he took up his troops
In youth his arrows pierced a standing shield
Beneath the robe of a gentle scholar
A warrior strength was breathing

[Chorus]
The open heart that shared his granary
Grew into a great design for the realm
People laughed and called him simple
Yet he saw farther than them all
He bound the alliance, took up command
He held up the realm in silence, Lu Su

[Bridge]
In the tales he wears a clumsy face
Going back and forth between two heroes
But sharing one granary, he saw a man worth
And never turned from the heaviest load
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Even that gap was his generous heart

[Outro]
The hand that shared the granary
Also held the brush that drew the realm

It was not simpleness but generosity
Not clumsiness but seeing far

Now at last I call it rightly
That name, Lu Su

[Outro Instrumental Ending]
[End]
```
