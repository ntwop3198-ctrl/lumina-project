## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Stream-enterer, they say he entered the stream
Yet there was nowhere he entered
Once-returner, they say he comes and goes once
Yet there is no coming or going

[Verse 2]
Non-returner, they say he will not return
Yet there is no not returning
If an arhat says, I am an arhat
He is already not an arhat

[Pre-Chorus]
There is a name
But no seat to go with it

[Chorus]
A prize with no form
Received without knowing it was received
Called the first
Yet never thinking himself first
A prize with no form
And so the highest prize

[Verse 3]
The Buddha said of Subhuti
First among those who live without strife
But Subhuti did not keep
Those words inside his heart

[Pre-Chorus]
There is a name
But no seat to go with it

[Chorus]
A prize with no form
Received without knowing it was received
Called the first
Yet never thinking himself first
A prize with no form
And so the highest prize

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
An old zelkova at the village gate, not a single award
Became the shade for the whole village
There is a name
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
And so the highest prize

[Chorus]
A prize with no form
Received without knowing it was received
Called the first
Yet never thinking himself first
A prize with no form
And so the highest prize

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Because Subhuti truly does nothing at all
He is called one who delights in dwelling in peace
```
