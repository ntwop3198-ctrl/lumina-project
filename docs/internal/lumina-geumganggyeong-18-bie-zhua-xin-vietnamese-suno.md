## Style

```
Vietnamese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, calm with a gentle smile, flowing, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Vietnamese vocal, 72 BPM, Vietnamese only
```

nhà sư cứng họng
chẳng ăn được bánh, quay đi
