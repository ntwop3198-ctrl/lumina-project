## Style

```
Circus polka with oom-pah tuba, brisk accordion and goofy kazoo slides, brisk bouncy 2/4, theatrical male vocal with playful ad-libs and a short slapback vocal echo, Vietnamese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, hip hop, autotune, heavy metal, sad ballad, dramatic belting, female vocal
```
