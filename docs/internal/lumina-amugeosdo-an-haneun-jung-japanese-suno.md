Japanese folk blues, very slow lazy drawl at 72 BPM, low vocal opening, low acoustic slide guitar, brushed upright bass, muted harmonica answers, deep male Japanese baritone spoken-sung, restrained within the verse range, close warm room production, simple thick harmony, chorus gently rising, one weary harmonica phrase fading out, short Korean chant in bridge
