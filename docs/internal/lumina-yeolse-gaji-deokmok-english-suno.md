## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
A young man barely twenty
Opened up a little book
Thirteen virtues one by one
Line by line he wrote them down

[Verse 2]
Temperance, Silence, Order, Resolution
Frugality, Industry, Sincerity, Justice
Moderation, Cleanliness, Tranquility, Chastity
And last of all Humility

[Pre-Chorus]
On a day he slipped, a small black spot
In every square he marked it down

[Chorus]
Thirteen virtues
What good shall I do today
What good have I done today
Every day he asked himself
Shaping not the others but himself
Franklin

[Interlude]

[Verse 3]
Scraping out and scraping out
Till the paper wore through full of holes
He drew it new on ivory leaves
And wiped them with a wet sponge

[Verse 4]
Order, he confessed, to the end
Was the one he could not keep
Humility he only wore
In appearance, and he laughed

[Pre-Chorus]
A speckled axe not ground quite bright
He smiled and said it was enough

[Chorus]
Thirteen virtues
What good shall I do today
What good have I done today
Every day he asked himself
Shaping not the others but himself
Franklin

[Bridge]
Though he never grew a perfect saint
He wrote that trying made him better still
One small book, one black spot was enough
The shame of a failing day he did not turn away
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
With a clearer face than yesterday he fills the square of today

[Final Chorus]
Thirteen virtues
What good shall I do today
What good have I done today
Every day he asked himself
Shaping not the others but himself
Franklin

[Outro]
Thirteen virtues
Today again
Quietly one square

[End]
```
