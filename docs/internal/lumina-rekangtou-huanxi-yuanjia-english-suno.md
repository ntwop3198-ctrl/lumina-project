## Style

```
Errenzhuan Northeast China folk song, featured banhu fiddle, festive suona and rapid bamboo clappers, dry percussion with sharp transients, lively 120 BPM pulse, comedic male-female dialogue in warm raspy gravelly mid-range voices, English vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Male]
Oh my, snow outside, a warm kang inside
These good days feel just right
[Female]
Feel right, my foot, you are a hands-off boss
[Verse 1]
[Male]
Listen here, dear mother of my kids
Snow blocks the door, we curl up inside
On the warm kang I sit cross-legged
A pipe of tobacco, I live like a god
[Female]
You a god and me a millstone
No wood chopped, no water hauled
Every winter you flop on the kang
Look at you, like an old pumpkin ball
[Pre-Chorus]
[Male]
What now, it is called work and rest, you know
[Female]
Know what, the pickle jar is empty, will you move
[Male]
Freezing cold, what are you fussing about
[Female]
Without my fuss, grass would grow in your bones
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
Hankies and fans fly to the sky
Pots and pans bang and clatter
Noisy and lively, that is the New Year
You glare at me, hands on my hips
On the kang, neither one gives in
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
Bumps and all, worth more than gold
[Interlude]
[Verse 2]
[Male]
Dear, do not get mad
Tonight I stew the goose, I take the ladle
Noodles a-plenty, sauerkraut galore
A shot of white liquor, let us talk
[Female]
Sweet talk all day, you wind me round
What can I do with you, old fox
You pluck the goose, I put it in the pot
Eat up, then go sweep the snow
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
Hankies and fans fly to the sky
Pots and pans bang and clatter
Noisy and lively, that is the New Year
You glare at me, hands on my hips
On the kang, neither one gives in
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
Bumps and all, worth more than gold
[Bridge]
[Female]
Half a life of fights, neither can let go
[Male]
You hold the ladle, I feed the fire
[Female]
Good days do not fall from the sky
[Male]
Two pairs of hands build a good life
[Duet]
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Right here on the warm kang, the good life
[Outro]
[Male]
Dear, is the goose done yet
[Female]
The smell filled the whole village, grab the chopsticks
[Duet]
Ha ha ha ha ha
Our days are steaming hot
Even the gods would be jealous
[Fade Out]
```
