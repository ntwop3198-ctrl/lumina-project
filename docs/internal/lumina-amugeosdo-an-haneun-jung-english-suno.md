## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
Rolling around since the morning
Lazing about past noon
I'm not idle 'cause there's nothing to do
Today I just decided not to

[Pre-Chorus]
The voice that calls me lucky or calls me a loafer
I chose not to carry from the start

[Chorus]
Rolling around, lazing about
The sun crawls slowly up the wall
All the things a busy man would never see
There were so many right here in my room

[Verse 2]
So that's the shape of the stain on the ceiling
At three in the afternoon the wardrobe corner glows
The yellow dog next door always barks at four
Ten years living here and I only know it now

[Bridge]
구소구고구기
Taking less, enduring, setting it all down
진공묘유
When I stop forcing, everything runs just right

[Final Chorus]
Rolling around, lazing about
Today I'm sprawled out just like this
No need to brag that I'm resting right
No need to feel sorry for what I didn't do

[Outro]
Doing nothing right now
Please don't disturb me
It's going really well right now
```
