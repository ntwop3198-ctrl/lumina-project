## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
You only loved the sand fish hiding below
Why did I never get a single song
Named for the willow leaf, the willow minnow
I only live in the clearest water

[Verse 2]
When you crouched by the stream as a kid
And tossed me a grain of corn rice
I was the first to dart right over
Looking you in the eye, that was me

[Pre-Chorus]
Swimming upstream with a silver flash
Wiggling, flicking my tail all day
Even if you grew up and forgot
My little fins remember you

[Chorus]
Hey, sing a fine song for me too
Fastest of the Yanbian stream, the willow minnow
Like a fairy in the rocks, splashing along
The brave captain of the clear shallows
Willow minnow, willow minnow, do not forget me

[Verse 3]
Years roll on, city dust piles high
But in the spring of your heart I still swim
In muddy water I will not stay a second
That clear and honest heart is me

[Bridge]
Emptied out, a place grown clear
In the clearest stream is where I live
One grain of rice and I race over
Strong currents, I never turn back
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
If it is not clear water, I will not stay

[Chorus]
Hey, sing a fine song for me too
Fastest of the Yanbian stream, the willow minnow
Like a fairy in the rocks, splashing along
The brave captain of the clear shallows
Willow minnow, willow minnow, do not forget me

[Outro]
Sand fish, come on out of the sand
Let us ride the clear water and play
[Splash]
```
