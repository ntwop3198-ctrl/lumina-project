## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Gentle acoustic guitar and warm cello]

[Verse 1]
Falling out of trees and tumbling off rocks
Back when I had no fear I didn't even feel the pain
The man who'd dive into rough water without a doubt
Now grows careful in front of his daughter

[Verse 2]
The day I swam fifty meters out into the deep sea
Daddy growing smaller and smaller in my princess's eyes
Afraid that shrinking daddy might never come back
I can still see my princess's face as she cried her heart out

[Pre-Chorus]
To me it was just a proud and daring challenge
But to you it was a fear that had no end

[Chorus]
I'm sorry my daughter for being a daddy with no fear
That moment I vanished from your sight still weighs on my heart
Daddy wasn't scared at all
But the fear you felt was my fault after all

[Interlude]
[Warm cello and soft piano]

[Verse 3]
Now I'm older and my body's not what it was
But my heart is still the same as back then
Even when I fall and get hurt I laugh it off
But worrying for my daughter hasn't changed at all

[Bridge]
[Gradually building emotion]
Now my princess worries daddy will get into trouble
And daddy still wants to live without fear
Inside our different kinds of worry
I know it was the very same love

[Chorus]
I'm sorry my daughter for being a daddy with no fear
That moment I vanished from your sight still weighs on my heart
Daddy wasn't scared at all
But the fear you felt was my fault after all

[Outro]
Today again daddy falls and gets back up
And my princess watches with worried eyes
I'm sorry and thank you my daughter
You'll always be daddy's greatest treasure
[Fade out with lingering acoustic guitar]
```
