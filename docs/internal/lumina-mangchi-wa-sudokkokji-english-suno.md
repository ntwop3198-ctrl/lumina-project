## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Verse 1]
One night when I was seven
A faucet dripping drip drop
My brother said tap it with a hammer
A few taps and it will stop
[Verse 2]
I believed every word he said
Took the hammer, tiptoed in
Tap once, tap twice, softly
Then bang, I swung it hard
[Pre-Chorus]
And right then, whoosh
Water shot up to the ceiling
[Chorus]
The hammer and the faucet
The whole house a sea
My brother and I in a panic
Just stamping our feet
The hammer and the faucet
That night of the flood
[Interlude]
[Verse 3]
We knocked to wake the neighbor
He ran with a shutoff hose
The whole family hung on
Till at last the water stopped
[Pre-Chorus]
Wiping up the soaking floor
Nobody scolded me at all
[Bridge]
That night nobody blamed a soul
The grown-ups laughed it all away
It is fine to make mistakes
A quiet hand patted my back
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
I will hug the mistakes of others with a smile
[Final Chorus]
The hammer and the faucet
The whole house a sea
My brother and I in a panic
Just stamping our feet
The hammer and the faucet
That night of the flood
[Outro]
When I see a dripping faucet
That night comes back and makes me laugh
[Fade Out]
```
