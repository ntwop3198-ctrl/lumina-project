## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
By a body perfect and complete
can the Tathagata be seen
No, World-Honored One
not by a perfect body

[Verse 2]
A perfect body is not a perfect body
it only bears that name
the thirty-two noble marks as well
by those marks he cannot be seen

[Pre-Chorus]
Cast it in gold
or carve it from wood

[Chorus]
No form, no shape at all
seek with the eyes and it slips away
wrap it in gold and it is hidden
No form, no shape at all
and so it is everywhere
right inside the eye that sees

[Verse 3]
[Playful storytelling]
Snow fell on the temple and the cold crept in
Master Danxia burned a wooden Buddha for a fire
the abbot leapt up in alarm
he poked the ashes, looking for relics, he said
what relics could a piece of wood hold
then it's only wood, let's warm our hands

[Pre-Chorus]
Cast it in gold
or carve it from wood

[Chorus]
No form, no shape at all
seek with the eyes and it slips away
wrap it in gold and it is hidden
No form, no shape at all
and so it is everywhere
right inside the eye that sees

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
even a golden face fades with the years
in the fire that warms cold hands, the Buddha
Cast it in gold
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
right inside the eye that sees

[Chorus]
No form, no shape at all
seek with the eyes and it slips away
wrap it in gold and it is hidden
No form, no shape at all
and so it is everywhere
right inside the eye that sees

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
The perfect body the Tathagata speaks of is not a perfect body
so it is called the perfect body
```
