## Title

```
No Dharma to Teach
```

부제: Diamond Sutra Chapter 21, What Is Spoken Is Not Spoken  
문서 한글: **설할 법이 없다** · 보컬 **남성** · 따뜻하고 차분하게

```
English acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, quiet and spacious with long pauses, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male English vocal, 68 BPM, English only
```

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Do not think the Tathagata
has a Dharma he must teach
to say the Tathagata taught a Dharma
slanders me, not knowing what I mean

[Verse 2]
To teach the Dharma
is to have no Dharma to teach
and this is what we name
the teaching of the Dharma

[Pre-Chorus]
There is a mouth that speaks
but no Dharma to be taught

[Chorus]
No Dharma to teach
forty-five years of teaching
yet he says not one word was taught
No Dharma to teach
in the heart of the one who hears
it was all there from the start

[Verse 3]
That very first morning
he washed his feet, spread his seat and sat
not a single word was spoken
yet all of it was taught

[Pre-Chorus]
There is a mouth that speaks
but no Dharma to be taught

[Chorus]
No Dharma to teach
forty-five years of teaching
yet he says not one word was taught
No Dharma to teach
in the heart of the one who hears
it was all there from the start

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
where the words come to an end
one wordless strain of the bamboo flute
There is a mouth that speaks
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
it was all there from the start

[Chorus]
No Dharma to teach
forty-five years of teaching
yet he says not one word was taught
No Dharma to teach
in the heart of the one who hears
it was all there from the start

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
To teach the Dharma is to have no Dharma to teach
that is called teaching the Dharma
```
