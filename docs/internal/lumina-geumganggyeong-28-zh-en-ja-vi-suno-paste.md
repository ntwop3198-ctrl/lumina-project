```
莫贪福德
```

```

```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]

不如知道无我

菩萨为何不受福德
因为对所作的福德
不贪也不执着

虽有所作的福
却没有去收的手

[Chorus]
莫贪福德

脚步才轻
莫贪福德

[Verse 3]
[Warm acoustic strum, reminiscent and tender]

树梢上留了几个
说这是留给喜鹊的
没有全部摘下

[Pre-Chorus]
虽有所作的福
却没有去收的手

[Chorus]
莫贪福德
做了的就让它在那里
不想收获而走的人
脚步才轻
莫贪福德
别全摘光 留一些

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
没有摘尽的枝头上
一对喜鹊飞来停下
虽有所作的福
진공묘유
求小求苦求己, 不向外求
别全摘光 留一些

[Chorus]
莫贪福德
做了的就让它在那里
不想收获而走的人
脚步才轻
莫贪福德
```

```
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

More than a bodhisattva who fills
worlds like Ganges sands with treasure to give
the one who knows there is no self

[Verse 2]
Subhuti asked the Buddha
why does a bodhisattva not receive merit
because for the merit he has made
he holds no craving, no clinging

[Pre-Chorus]
There is merit that was made
but no hand to take it

[Chorus]

walks with a lighter step
Do not crave merit
don't pick it all, leave some behind

[Verse 3]

left a few up at the very top
that's for the magpies, he would say
and never picked them all

but no hand to take it

[Chorus]
Do not crave merit
let what you've done stay as it is
one who walks on with no thought of harvest
walks with a lighter step
Do not crave merit

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
on the branch not stripped bare

[Chorus]
Do not crave merit
```
