## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro: bamboo flute and pipa]

[Verse 1]
Willows at the gate turn with another autumn
This time last year I was waiting too
The words came to my lips but I swallowed them down
And only said it's cold another autumn's come

[Verse 2]
I have no grand gate no jade tower
Just half a boat of clear moonlight and a pole
If you ask what I can promise you
Only a lifetime of never looking back

[Pre-Chorus]
I don't dare ask what's in your heart
I only dare ask will you or won't you

[Chorus]
Tell me when will you come away with me
To where the waters of Jiangnan end
No gold no jade no lofty hall
Only a lifetime side by side
Tell me when will you come away with me
Don't let the autumn leaves turn our hair white
The rarest thing in this world is
Someone willing to share your boat

[Verse 3]
You needn't answer me tonight
Take your time there's no need to worry
I only fear when next year's willows turn green
There'll be no one waiting here

[Bridge: instruments thin to flute and voice]
I'm not a shooting star's brief flash
Gone without a trace once it's passed
I'm a single lamp through the long night
Once it's lit it stays till dawn

[Final Chorus: strings enter, fuller]
Tell me when will you come away with me
To where the waters of Jiangnan end
No gold no jade no lofty hall
Only a lifetime side by side
Tell me when will you come away with me
Don't let the autumn leaves turn our hair white
The rarest thing in this world is
Someone willing to share your boat

[Outro: bamboo flute alone, warm fade]
Tell me
Will you
Come with me
```
