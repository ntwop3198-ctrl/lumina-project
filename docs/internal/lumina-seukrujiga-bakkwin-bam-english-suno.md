[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Foggy London streets on a frozen Christmas Eve
Lamps in every window, laughter blooming bright
But Scrooge kept his room like a bolted iron gate
He called Christmas humbug and slammed the door shut tight

[Pre-Chorus]
The shadow of old Marley, dragging heavy chains
Three spirits came to call upon the bitter wind

The night he was born again, a brand-new man
