## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Hold this sutra, read it and recite
and if you are looked down upon
the heavy karma of lives before
is washed away by this

[Verse 2]
Before the Buddha Dipankara came
offerings were made to countless Buddhas
yet next to the merit of holding this sutra
not one part in a hundred

[Pre-Chorus]
Even being scorned
is never in vain

[Chorus]
Washed by faith, the karma clears
one sharp word, one layer of old grime
one mocking laugh, another layer
washed and washed until it's clear
Washed by faith, the karma clears
white as linen on the line

[Verse 3]
Every dawn a hundred and eight bows
they scoff, you'll only ruin your knees
he smiles and bows down once again
and his back grows light

[Pre-Chorus]
Even being scorned
is never in vain

[Chorus]
Washed by faith, the karma clears
one sharp word, one layer of old grime
one mocking laugh, another layer
washed and washed until it's clear
Washed by faith, the karma clears
white as linen on the line

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
the more you beat and rinse, the whiter the cloth
cloth already white, with nothing to wash
Even being scorned
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
white as linen on the line

[Chorus]
Washed by faith, the karma clears
one sharp word, one layer of old grime
one mocking laugh, another layer
washed and washed until it's clear
Washed by faith, the karma clears
white as linen on the line

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
The karma of former lives is washed away
the meaning is beyond thought, and so is its fruit
```
