[Intro]
[Guitar Shuffle]
[Harmonica]

[Verse 1]
Since we were kids they called him Sage
Not because he knew a thing
Was it the way he talked so slow
Or just the look upon his face

Never seen him meditate

Because he wants nothing
Everything I hold comes out

And nobody there
Wants anything from me

How easy that feels
Only those who know it know

[Chorus]
Sage, let us have a drink

Is that house still standing there
Not bragging about the glory days
Just the days we spent together
For those who have a lot of those
That alone is enough

[Bridge]
[Guitar Only]
[Spoken]
