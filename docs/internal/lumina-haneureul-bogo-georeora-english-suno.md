## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Spoken]
Long, long ago, deep in the mountains, there is an amazing tale
[Verse 1]
A ginseng hunter famed for a hundred miles
Every trip up the mountain, thick roots came home
Every storehouse piled high with sacks of rice
No one around had not heard his name
[Verse 2]
In that village lived a simple young man
Wanting to save a proper wedding fund
Every day he trailed the hunter like a shadow
Begging him to share just one secret
[Pre-Chorus]
Fed up with him, the hunter
Tossed a line just to fool him
If you want wild ginseng, go deep in the hills
Look nowhere else, just walk looking at the sky
[Chorus]
Walk looking at the sky, walk looking at the sky
He took that line word for word
Through the morning dew, wandering mountain trails
Neck aching, only looking at the sky
Walk looking at the sky
The words tossed out as a joke
[Verse 3]
Tripping on stones, walking all day long
At dusk he stood before a giant old tree
High up in a hollow of the trunk
A hundred-year ginseng waved its red berries
[Verse 4]
Sold it, built a tile-roofed house as big as a whale
Married a lovely bride, kids, a good life
Only the hunter who tossed that line
Kicked his blanket every night, green with envy
[Bridge]
Eyes that only swept the ground all his life
Would never find that treetop, look as he might
One small joke of a line
At the end of a day of stumbling falls
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Changed the fate of a simple young man
[Chorus]
Walk looking at the sky, walk looking at the sky
He took that line word for word
Through the morning dew, wandering mountain trails
Neck aching, only looking at the sky
Walk looking at the sky
The words tossed out as a joke
[Outro]
[Spoken]
After that, they say the old hunter in the hills
Also craned his neck and stared at the sky
Life is a funny thing, you never know
[Fade Out]

```
