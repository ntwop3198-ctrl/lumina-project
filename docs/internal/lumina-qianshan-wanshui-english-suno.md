## Title

```
Across a Thousand Hills
```

문서 한글: **산 넘고 물 건너도** · 보컬 **남성** — 따뜻하고 구수한 톤, 1절 기차역에서 목이 메듯 올라갔다가 2절은 통화하며 웃듯 풀어지게

---

## Style

```
English campus folk ballad, warm male vocal, mid-tempo gentle sway, warm analog tape saturation, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, dizi and soft harmonica, light brushed drums and gentle strings, English vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

---

## Exclude Styles

```
EDM, trap, rap, heavy drums, autotune, belting, rock guitar, Cantonese, female vocal
```

고급 설정: 기이함 **20%** · 스타일 영향 **70%** · 다양성 보통 · 개인화 끔

수노 v6 맨 위 곡 설명 칸은 **비운다.**

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Acoustic Guitar]
[Dizi]

[Verse 1]
That year in the dorm, bunk beds stacked
We lined up by age, number this, number that
One pack of smokes passed around
One bottle shared by all

Graduation day at the station
The whistle blew, we knocked on the windows
Running with the train, calling names
We cried out loud, all of us

[Pre-Chorus]
Hills beyond the hills
Rivers beyond the rivers

[Chorus]
Across a thousand hills, our bond remains
However far, our hearts stay the same
On the phone, one word, brother
Like back then, never apart

[Verse 2]
You teach up in the north
He trades down in the south
And I am on this side of the sea
Writing songs one by one

Hair gone white, bellies grown round
On video calls we tease and laugh
You rascal, still the same old you
Open your mouth and youth comes back

[Pre-Chorus]
Hills beyond the hills
Rivers beyond the rivers

[Chorus]
Across a thousand hills, our bond remains
However far, our hearts stay the same
On the phone, one word, brother
Like back then, never apart

[Bridge]
No need to meet so often
No need to call so much
One are you well is enough
When I ache to see you, I let it be
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
A thousand hills, and all of them are near

[Final Chorus]
Across a thousand hills, our bond remains
However far, our hearts stay the same
On the phone, one word, brother
Like back then, never apart

[Outro]
[Dizi Fade]
Across a thousand hills, our bond remains
Like back then, never apart
[Fade Out]
[End]
```
