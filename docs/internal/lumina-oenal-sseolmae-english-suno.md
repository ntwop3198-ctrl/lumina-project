## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Winter Wind]
[Ice Scraping]
[Erhu]

[Verse 1]
Every winter the river froze
All the village kids came out
Riding sleds across the ice
But I had none
Father came home from the school
With one old skate in hand
Took the iron off and nailed it to a board
Then whittled two ice sticks for me

[Pre-Chorus]
Not a sled with two runners
It was one runner only

[Chorus]
One-runner sled
Put one foot up first
Brace with the ice sticks
Then bring up the other
Not leaning this way or that
Hold your breath and find your balance
Only then would I glide away
On the runner my father made

[Verse 2]
At first I fell more times than I could count
My backside black and blue
Still I climbed back on
Put one foot up first
After a few days
My body knew before me
Where to put my strength
So I would not fall

[Pre-Chorus]
Back then I did not know
Who had sharpened that iron

[Chorus]
One-runner sled
From the school storeroom
One old runner
Became my winter
Father kept the school accounts
I was the youngest of the house
And the iron, cut down
Was fixed beneath my feet

[Bridge]
[Guitar Only]
Now I see those hands
Hands that cut the steel and drove the nails
Hands that smoothed each ice stick tip
Bruised all winter, I still climbed back on
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Eyes that saw only the sled now see those hands

[Final Chorus]
One-runner sled
Now I ride on wheels
Rolling along Dorimcheon
Sometimes I balance on one foot
Not leaning this way or that
When I push down with my toes
I remember that ice
On the runner my father made

[Outro]
[Erhu]
[Guitar Single Note]
One foot first
Then the other
That is how I learned
```
