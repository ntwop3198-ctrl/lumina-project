## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft acoustic guitar, low piano]

[Verse 1]
The reason life feels heavy is usually simple
We hold on to everything we never really needed
The load in our hands may look like a lot
But what we truly need was never that much

[Verse 2]
Desire is simple at its root, when thirsty we want water
When hungry we want food, and that is all
But we reach past need for richer taste and finer clothes
The more we cling to what we don't need, the heavier life grows

[Chorus]
The moment we want less, life begins to change
Want just as much as I need, and life feels so much lighter
Owning more may seem to bring us comfort

[Chorus]
```
