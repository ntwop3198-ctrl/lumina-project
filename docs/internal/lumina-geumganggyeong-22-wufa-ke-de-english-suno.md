## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Subhuti asked the Buddha
when you gained awakening
was it because nothing was gained
So it is, so it is, he said

[Verse 2]
In that awakening
I gained not even a speck of dust
because nothing was gained
it is called awakening

[Pre-Chorus]
What I was searching for
was there from the start

[Chorus]
Nothing to gain
no Dharma to teach and no Dharma to gain
what was always yours
where would you go to gain it
Nothing to gain
and so you have it all

[Verse 3]
[Playful storytelling, gentle acoustic bounce]
All morning I hunted for my glasses
turned the whole house upside down
then I stood before the mirror
and there they were upon my nose

[Pre-Chorus]
What I was searching for
was there from the start

[Chorus]
Nothing to gain
no Dharma to teach and no Dharma to gain
what was always yours
where would you go to gain it
Nothing to gain
and so you have it all

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
the farther I searched, the farther it went
where I stopped, it had been all along
What I was searching for
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
and so you have it all

[Chorus]
Nothing to gain
no Dharma to teach and no Dharma to gain
what was always yours
where would you go to gain it
Nothing to gain
and so you have it all

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Not even the smallest dharma can be gained
this is called Anuttara Samyak Sambodhi
```
