Someone told me every time we meet there's one less left
How many more times do you think we'll get to see each other
If you count it out they're right
But I decided not to count that way

[Verse 2]
We weren't all that close when we were young
For a while we forgot each other and lived our lives
Somehow we met again in this hard-edged city
And now it's twice or so a week

[Pre-Chorus]
It's not that we're thirsty for a drink
It's not that we've got so much to say

That's how we've come this far

[Bridge]
Some things you know without a word

Took us such a long long time
