## Lyrics (v1)

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]
They all called him a young scholar
Yet that year he was thirty-eight
Even Liu Bei took him lightly
Laughed him off as a man of books

But Sun Quan saw the depth in his eyes
And placed the chief command in his hands
Behind the boyish face the novel drew
Stood a seasoned mind who knew the field

[Verse 2]
When Liu Bei army surged along the shore
The generals begged to march and fight
He held the gate and answered low
He said he would not move before the time

In the summer heat the Shu camps stood in rows
He saw the fences bound of wood and grass
And knew at last the hour for fire had come
Seven hundred li of camps went up in flame

[Chorus]
He did not hurry so he won at last
They looked down on him so he proved it more
The novel called him a pale-faced scholar
The record wrote he waited and he won
The one who moved the last
Was the one who smiled the most, Lu Xun

[Verse 3]
When the fires of Yiling had faded
The northern host came to the border again
Cao Xiu was lured by a false surrender
And broken in the valley of Shiting

Two great battles, two long waits
What he guarded was the sky of Jiangdong
The name they scorned as just a scholar
Became the name of a shining win

[Chorus]
He did not hurry so he won at last

[Bridge]
At glory end he found no peace
In court disputes his will was left undone
In one fence in a row he saw the time
He did not flee the hard and lonely years

[Outro]
The seven hundred li of fire, the win at Shiting
