## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Daegeum]
There is a ferry by the river
Coming and going, feet rest awhile

[Verse 1]
A ferry landing by the river
A place where travelers rest
A waterway held in one name
Even shaking, it stands there today

[Pre-Chorus]
Every story is different
The landing makes room down low
Leave your worries to the water
Just wet your feet and cross

[Chorus]
강진 강진 강진
Kang for river, jin for ferry
Rest awhile, all who cross
A landing standing low
강진 강진 강진
The river flows, the landing stays
Same place today, a ferry landing

[Verse 2]
Some cross over, some stay
Each sets down one load
The river flows, the landing stays the same
Today it keeps its place

[Bridge]
Finding a place by the low water
Big boat or small, it does not choose
Wet feet can rest here too
Strong current, it keeps its place
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
In the empty landing, people stay

[Chorus]
강진 강진 강진
Kang for river, jin for ferry
Rest awhile, all who cross
A landing standing low
강진 강진 강진
Even shaking, it keeps its place today
Just as it is, a ferry landing

[Outro]
[Daegeum]
강진, 강진
Same place today
A ferry landing
[Fade Out]
```
