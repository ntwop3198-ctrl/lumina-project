## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
A night of pitch-black darkness settles down
Even with my eyes closed, I can't see the road
The world demands that I show it some proof
And tells me to doubt a future I can't see

[Pre-Chorus]
Can you see tomorrow's sun before your eyes right now
Someone softly threw that question my way
I quietly laid my hand on my heart and smiled without a sound
Even though we can't see it, we already know

[Chorus]
You have to believe first, then at last you see
Tomorrow's sun is already rising in our hearts
Even when the heavy fog stands in my way
I'll walk through today with steps of faith

[Verse 2]
The world says only trust what your eyes have seen
And stop the steps that walk without certainty
But miracles are always in the unseen place
Breathing quietly, waiting there for us

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Just as I never doubt the sun will rise again tomorrow
I ask myself and say it over and over
Before a shaking world there's only one answer
Because my heart already points to tomorrow

[Chorus]
You have to believe first, then at last you see
Tomorrow's sun is already rising in our hearts
Even when the heavy fog stands in my way
I'll walk through today with steps of faith

[Bridge]
When everyone warns me it's a road no one has walked
I hold excitement in place of fear
One step, then one more step
On the road that faith has made, the light at last comes down

[Interlude]
[Grand and hopeful instrumental, guitar solo]

[Chorus - Key Up]
Because I believed first, at last I see it face to face
That promised morning lights the world so bright
All the hearts that never gave up gather as one
And unfold before my eyes by the name of miracle

[Outro]
The darkness fades, the sun is rising
Even though I couldn't see
I believed, and so at last I saw
[Gentle lingering fade out]
```
