## Title

```
Market Day in the Snow
```

문서 원본: **雪天赶大集** · 보컬 **남녀 듀엣** — 들뜬 남편과 핀잔 주는 아내의 빠른 주고받기, 고음까지 시원하게 뻗는 톤, 뒤에 받쳐 주는 코러스

## Style

```
Errenzhuan Northeast China folk song, 124 BPM brisk syncopated bounce, close-miked bamboo clapper transients, comedic male-female duet, quick bantering call-and-response, wide-range lines climbing into top notes with backing voices, lively suona flourishes and bouncy banhu fiddle, English vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

```
EDM, rap, heavy metal, autotune, trap, slow ballad
```

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Male]
Oh my, this snowstorm blowing
My eyelashes froze into icicles
Hey dear, walk a little faster
[Female]
Stop rushing, the road is slick as a slide
If I fall flat on my back, you kneel on the washboard at home
[Verse 1]
[Male]
The northwest wind goes whoo whoo
Pulling our little sled, we push through the snow
Dog fur hat on my head
Big cotton boots crunch crunch in the snow
Our corn sold for a good price this year
With the mother of my kids, off to buy New Year goods
[Female]
Look at you walking like a clumsy bear
Pulling an empty sled and huffing away
Two coins in your pocket and you strut
Do not sneak off to old Li for a drink on the way
When buying pork, pick the lower belly
Do not grab the cheap stuff and bring home a bag of bones
[Pre-Chorus]
[Male]
Hey dear, I want frozen pears and candied haws
[Female]
Eat eat eat, freeze off your front teeth, then what will you chew
[Male]
And I will get three feet of red cloth for your flower coat
[Female]
Oh stop it, at my age, dressed up like a mountain flower
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
Hankies and fans fly to the sky
The wind whistles, snowflakes drift
The market is packed, people pressed to people
Frozen pears, frozen persimmons, red haws
Sticky bean buns steaming right into your throat
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
Lively and loud at the market
Spinning home to a red-hot New Year
[Interlude]
[Verse 2]
[Male]
At the market gate, what a bustle
The big iron pot of stew sends up smoke
Two ounces of grain liquor for dad
A pair of golden New Year pictures for mom to hang
[Female]
Two big candied haw sticks for the kids
And fox fur earmuffs for you, dear
A whole year sweating in the fields
Today we buy freely, nobody skimps
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
Hankies and fans fly to the sky
The wind whistles, snowflakes drift
The market is packed, people pressed to people
Frozen pears, frozen persimmons, red haws
Sticky bean buns steaming right into your throat
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
Lively and loud at the market
Spinning home to a red-hot New Year
[Bridge]
[Female]
Year round our sweat falls and splits in eight
[Male]
Every grain of corn saved by our hands
[Female]
No envy for others in gold and silk
[Male]
Our own New Year is warm just the same
[Duet]
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Hand in hand down the snowy road
[Outro]
[Male]
Oh dear, quick, give me a hand, I slipped
[Female]
Ha ha, serves you right, the sled landed on your bottom
Hurry up, tidy up, into the market we go
[Duet]
Ha ha ha ha ha
Snow everywhere, joy in every step
Red and bright, we greet the spring
[Fade Out]
```
