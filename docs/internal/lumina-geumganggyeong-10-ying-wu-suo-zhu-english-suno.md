## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Beside Dipankara Buddha
Was there some Dharma you attained
No, World Honored One
Truly nothing was attained

[Verse 2]
Does a bodhisattva adorn the Buddha lands
No, to adorn
Is not adorning
It is only called adorning

[Pre-Chorus]
Do not dwell on what is seen
Do not dwell on what is heard

[Chorus]
A mind that dwells nowhere
Give rise to the mind without dwelling anywhere
By light or by sound
A mind that is never caught
A mind that dwells nowhere
Just as it is, it is clear

[Verse 3]
A woodcutter came to market with firewood on his back
Passing an inn, he heard a single line
Give rise to the mind without dwelling anywhere
And right there, his eyes were opened

[Pre-Chorus]
Do not dwell on what is seen
Do not dwell on what is heard

[Chorus]
A mind that dwells nowhere
Give rise to the mind without dwelling anywhere
By light or by sound
A mind that is never caught
A mind that dwells nowhere
Just as it is, it is clear

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
Flowing water never pools in one place
Never pooling, always clear
Do not dwell on what is seen
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
Just as it is, it is clear

[Chorus]
A mind that dwells nowhere
Give rise to the mind without dwelling anywhere
By light or by sound
A mind that is never caught
A mind that dwells nowhere
Just as it is, it is clear

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Do not give rise to a mind that dwells on form, nor on sound, smell, taste, touch, or thought
Give rise to a mind that dwells nowhere
```
