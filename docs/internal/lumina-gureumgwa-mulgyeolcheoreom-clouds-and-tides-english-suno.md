## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[gentle waves rolling in, guzheng melody following]

[Verse 1 - Memory of the Island]
A lonely isle off Wenzhou's shore the path we walked as one
Carved into the rock a couplet we found it just by chance
"云朝朝朝朝朝朝朝散"
The clouds gather every morning then scatter once again

[Verse 2 - The Matching Line]
"潮長長長長長長長消"
The tide keeps rising higher then slowly ebbs away
One word turned seven times each reading something new
At that clever little game we laughed the whole way through

[Chorus]
Clouds come together and drift apart the waves roll in and go
Between two hearts how could it always stay so calm
The rough words said over wine were just a passing wave
What rises back like the tide is our bond that never fades

[Verse 3 - Already Behind Us]
For days your big brother carried all his sorry weight
But you brushed it off like you knew it all along
Like the blue sky behind the clouds is always there
Through all our clumsy clashes our friendship quietly stayed

[Chorus]
Clouds come together and drift apart the waves roll in and go

[Bridge]
Raised on the Wenzhou wind my steady little brother
Like the words carved on the old pillar of Jiangxin Temple
Though the years may wear us down our friendship won't wear thin
Slipped softly into a song your brother's shy and honest heart

[Outro]
Someday let's walk that Wenzhou sea again
On that island by that rock let's clink our glasses and laugh
Clouds may go but the sky remains you and me just like that
[fade out with guzheng melody and gentle waves]
```
