无有定法

## EN · No Fixed Awakening

No Fixed Awakening

[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
This time the Buddha asked first
Subhuti, has the Tathagata attained awakening
Is there some Dharma
The Tathagata has taught

[Verse 2]
Subhuti answered
As I understand it
There is no fixed Dharma called awakening
And no fixed Dharma for the Tathagata to teach

[Pre-Chorus]

It cannot be spoken

[Chorus]
There is no fixed awakening
There is no fixed Dharma
Nothing to call the Dharma
Nothing to call not Dharma
There is no fixed awakening
Each goes by their own way

[Verse 3]
Grandma's soybean paste had no written recipe
A handful here, a glance there, a little different every year
Yet it always tasted like Grandma
Ask her how she made it, and she would only smile

[Pre-Chorus]
It cannot be grasped
It cannot be spoken

[Chorus]
There is no fixed awakening
There is no fixed Dharma
Nothing to call the Dharma
Nothing to call not Dharma
There is no fixed awakening
Each goes by their own way

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
With no fixed road, every step becomes a road
With no fixed taste, each home has its own
It cannot be grasped
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
Each goes by their own way

[Chorus]
There is no fixed awakening
There is no fixed Dharma
Nothing to call the Dharma
Nothing to call not Dharma
There is no fixed awakening
Each goes by their own way

[Outro]
