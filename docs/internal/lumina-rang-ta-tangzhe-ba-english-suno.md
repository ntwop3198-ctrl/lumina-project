## Title

```
Let It Lie
```

## Style

```
Chinese folk comedy song, bouncy 112 BPM kuaiban pulse, male vocal with comic storytelling pauses, bright erhu lead licks over plucky pipa, woodblock, small gong accents and buoyant bass, kuaiban-pipa interlude with offbeat gong hits, short gong slapback, tea cup clink before the fade, English vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
A puddle of mud by the wall, sleeping sweet in the sun
Someone insists on plastering it up a high wall, ends with hands full of stinking mud
A dry rotten log in the backyard, quietly growing mold
Someone insists on carving it into a great beam, the chisel snaps again and again
[Pre-Chorus]
Oh my oh my, what are you fussing about
They are lying there cozy and just fine
[Chorus]
Let it lie, let it lie
Mud has the carefree joy of mud
Let it lie, let it lie
Rotten wood has its easy days
However big your truth, do not force it down their throat
Everyone walks their own road
Mind yourself and the world finds its peace
[Verse 2]
An old salted fish by the wok, too tired even to turn
Someone flips it back and forth, says it needs a chance to turn its life around
A big fat pig in the sty, eyes half closed, lost in thought
Someone comes with a kettle of boiling water, shouting to wake up its wits
[Pre-Chorus]
Oh my oh my, what is in it for you
Nobody ever asked you over
[Chorus]
Let it lie, let it lie
Mud has the carefree joy of mud
Let it lie, let it lie
Rotten wood has its easy days
However big your truth, do not force it down their throat
Everyone walks their own road
Mind yourself and the world finds its peace
[Interlude]
[Bridge]
Old Mencius said it long ago, the trouble with people is loving to teach
Not preaching, not teaching, not blocking the way, that too is great practice
When someone truly comes to ask, reaching out then is not too late
Each one has a fate of their own, let them run into the wall themselves
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
First sweep the snow before your own door, clean and clear
[Final Chorus]
Let it lie, let it lie
Salted fish has its calm and ease
Let it lie, let it lie
The old pig has its innocent dreams
However grand the sage truth, do not force it down their throat
Wide is the sky, wide is the land, each goes their way
Mind yourself and all the land is at peace
[Outro]
The mud sleeps, the log rests
The fish lies, the old pig thinks
As for me, one slurp of my big bowl of tea
[Fade Out]

```
