```
vintage classical French chanson style, warm accordion and gentle nylon guitar, soft upright bass, light brushed drums, old gramophone texture, bittersweet witty satire, dreamy and nostalgic mood, 84 bpm, Vietnamese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```
