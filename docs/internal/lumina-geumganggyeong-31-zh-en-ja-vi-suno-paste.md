## ZH · 法相也莫生

```
法相也莫生
```

```
Mandarin Chinese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, calm and open like a mountain summit, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Mandarin vocal, 70 BPM, Mandarin Chinese only

```

那人懂我的意思吗
不懂 世尊 他不懂

[Verse 2]
我见也好 人见也好
即非见 是名为见
发心求觉悟的人
应如是知 如是见 如是信

[Pre-Chorus]
知而不执
见而不刻

[Chorus]
法相也莫生

法相也莫生
只是名为法

[Verse 3]
[Warm and open, gentle acoustic strumming]
登上了山顶一看
插着一面旗
刻着已经征服
山却什么都不知道

[Pre-Chorus]
知而不执
见而不刻

[Chorus]
法相也莫生
说知道 就困在知里
说看见 就困在见里

只是名为法

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
一把拔下旗 下山的路上
整座山都是我的
知而不执
진공묘유
求小求苦求己, 不向外求
只是名为法

[Chorus]
法相也莫生
说知道 就困在知里
说看见 就困在见里
所说法相 即非法相
法相也莫生
只是名为法

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
于一切法 如是知 如是见 如是信解
不生法相
```

## EN · Make No Form of Dharma

```
Make No Form of Dharma
```

```
English acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, calm and open like a mountain summit, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male English vocal, 70 BPM, English only
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
If someone says the Buddha taught
a view of self, a view of others
does that person grasp my meaning
No, World-Honored One, he does not

[Verse 2]
A view of self, a view of others
is not a view, its name is view
one who sets the heart on awakening
should know thus, see thus, trust thus

[Pre-Chorus]
Know, but do not grasp
see, but do not carve it in

[Chorus]
Make no form of Dharma
say you know and you're caught in knowing
say you see and you're caught in seeing
the form called Dharma is not a form
Make no form of Dharma
it is only named the Dharma

[Verse 3]
[Warm and open, gentle acoustic strumming]
I climbed up to the mountaintop
and there a flag was planted
conquered, someone had carved
but the mountain never knew

[Pre-Chorus]
Know, but do not grasp
see, but do not carve it in

[Chorus]
Make no form of Dharma
say you know and you're caught in knowing
say you see and you're caught in seeing
the form called Dharma is not a form
Make no form of Dharma
it is only named the Dharma

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
I pull the flag and start back down
the whole mountain is mine
Know, but do not grasp
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
it is only named the Dharma

[Chorus]
Make no form of Dharma
say you know and you're caught in knowing
say you see and you're caught in seeing
the form called Dharma is not a form
Make no form of Dharma
it is only named the Dharma

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Toward all dharmas, know it thus, see it thus, trust it thus
and give rise to no form of Dharma
```

```

[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
仏が我という見方

いいえ わかっていません

[Verse 2]

このように知り このように見 このように信じよ

[Pre-Chorus]
知っても握りしめず
見ても刻みつけるな

[Chorus]
法という相もつくるな
知ったと言えば知ることに閉じこめられ
見たと言えば見ることに閉じこめられる
法と言った相も相ではない
法という相もつくるな
その名が法であるだけ

[Verse 3]
[Warm and open, gentle acoustic strumming]
山のてっぺんに登ってみると
旗が一本立っていた
征服したと刻んであるが
山は何も知らない

[Pre-Chorus]
知っても握りしめず
見ても刻みつけるな

[Chorus]
法という相もつくるな
知ったと言えば知ることに閉じこめられ
見たと言えば見ることに閉じこめられる
法と言った相も相ではない
法という相もつくるな
その名が法であるだけ

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
旗をぐいと抜いて下りる道
山はまるごとわたしのもの
知っても握りしめず
진공묘유
小を求め 苦を求め 己を求め, 外に求めず
その名が法であるだけ

[Chorus]
法という相もつくるな
知ったと言えば知ることに閉じこめられ
見たと言えば見ることに閉じこめられる
法と言った相も相ではない
法という相もつくるな

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
