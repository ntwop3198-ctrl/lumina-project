## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
In a sleeper car that rattles on its way
I lay my weary body down to rest
The winding road of life I've traveled
Slips past in the darkness outside the glass

[Verse 2]
There were days of wind and pouring rain
And days I laughed so bright and free
All the times I wandered and I fell
Have shaped the stronger me today

[Pre-Chorus]
Though the road ahead seems far away
The rails keep reaching on and on
So set your hurried steps aside
And calmly listen to the wind

[Chorus]
Sleeper train life, oh sleeper train life
Lie back easy, catch your breath
The rails already know the way
Don't be afraid because it sways
Sleeper train life, what a fine life it is
Carry your dreams on the midnight train
In the end I'll reach my place

[Verse 3]
Watching the morning star outside
I pat myself for all of today
When the dark lifts and the dawn comes in
How brightly will tomorrow shine

[Bridge]
With a heart that's lived through it all
I hold even the rattling sway
In the space I've let go and emptied
True rest at last becomes my strength
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
Tonight again I trust the rails

[Chorus]
Sleeper train life, oh sleeper train life
Lie back easy, catch your breath
The rails already know the way
Don't be afraid because it sways
Sleeper train life, what a fine life it is
Carry your dreams on the midnight train
In the end I'll reach my place

[Outro]
Tonight again on the rattling sleeper car
I carry my whole life and ride on proud
```
