## Style

```
Korean folk, close-miked pungmul percussion with crisp janggu and buk strokes, kkwaenggwari drives the pulse, bright taepyeongso accents and expressive haegeum fills, warm cheeky male lead with a rowdy folk choir, buoyant 100 BPM gutgeori swing, Vietnamese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```
