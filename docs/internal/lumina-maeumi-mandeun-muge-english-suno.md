## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Low strings]
I thought it was just an old saying
But living on, it stayed as weight

[Verse 1]
They say that everything begins in the mind
I passed it by as just a line of words
But when the days of a two-faced heart pile up
I have seen that weight settle somewhere

[Verse 2]
When the heart is not at ease, the night is not at ease
When the heart is heavy, so are my steps
The longer I hide and hold things down
The deeper that burden seeps somewhere

[Pre-Chorus]
It is not only the wind to blame
How I chose to use my heart
Can change the road beneath my feet
Though I do not know if that is all

[Chorus]
All is made by the mind, the mind makes its weight
A hidden heart weighs as much as it hides
An easy heart is as light as it rests
And that weight can linger in my steps
To tend my heart is to tend myself

[Verse 3]
It felt like I was fooling someone
But the one I fooled the longest was me
Now I listen a little to what my body says
I read the weight of my heart in how I walk

[Bridge]
What was I still trying to hold
I let some go and ask myself
I set down blame and look within
In the emptied space my breath grows calm
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
When the heart is light, the steps are light

[Chorus]
All is made by the mind, the mind makes its weight
A hidden heart weighs as much as it hides
An easy heart is as light as it rests
And that weight can linger in my steps
To tend my heart is to tend myself

[Outro]
[Lingering strings]
Look at the steps and you see the heart
Look at the heart and you see the life behind
```
