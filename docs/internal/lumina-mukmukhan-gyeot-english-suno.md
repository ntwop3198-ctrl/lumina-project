## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Low warm cello melody and plain acoustic guitar arpeggio]

[Verse 1]
Searching for some clumsy words of comfort
In the end I simply pressed my lips together
The weight of all the hard years you've come through
How could my small tongue ever measure it

[Verse 2]
Heavy words falling drop by drop
I quietly catch them in both hands before they hit the floor
In those eyes that never asked me for a fix
I know what you needed wasn't answers but an ear

[Pre-Chorus]
A careful night afraid any word might be a burden
I just match my breathing to the sound of yours

[Chorus]
Not judging and not trying to fix
Just someone who listens quietly with all their heart
On a night when the world feels so far away
Here one real person will stand for you whole
Pour it out every sigh knotted in your chest
I'll stay quietly by your side so I can hold it all

[Verse 3]
Even the sorrow you can't bring yourself to say
Even the knots that seep out between the silences
To listen quietly with no conditions at all
That's the deepest warmth that I can give

[Bridge]
구소구고구기
Not putting my thoughts first I empty out a space in my heart
진공묘유
In that emptied ground I lay your pain to rest
A silence greater than advice a company more precious than the right answer

[Chorus]
Not judging and not trying to fix
Just someone who listens quietly with all their heart
On a night when the world feels so far away
Here one real person will stand for you whole
Pour it out every sigh knotted in your chest
I'll stay quietly by your side so I can hold it all

[Outro]
[Long cello sustain, gentle guitar picking]
Until you've emptied it all until the night passes and morning comes
I'll stay right here quietly by your side
```
