## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Acoustic Guitar]
[Harmonica]

[Verse 1]
You only talk big in front of a glass
Talking fists until the morning light
You say you will help your brother
But the bill is always mine

[Verse 2]
Once the night is gone you forget it all
Next day, the same old seat
You know it is yours to carry
So why that seat again

[Pre-Chorus]
I do not say this out of hate
Today I am just tired too

[Chorus]
Wake up, little brother
Your brother is tired now
The people still beside you
Are leaving one by one
To the ones who held out a hand
Do not raise your fist
When everyone is gone
Who will call your name

[Interlude]

[Verse 3]
Put some cash in your hand, it is gone that day
So this time I will not give
When you find a room with your own two hands
Then I will be the first to come

[Bridge]
You said you could not raise a hand to me
And quietly turned away
If that one heart is still there
It is not too late
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Your road is yours to find

[Final Chorus]
Wake up, little brother
Your brother is tired now
The people still beside you
Are still waiting for you
To the ones who held out a hand
Just say thank you
That one word is enough
Start again from there

[Outro]
Wake up, little brother
Your brother is right here
[Fade Out]
```
