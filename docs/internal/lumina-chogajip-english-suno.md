## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
A roof of woven rice straw
We laid fresh every year
Mending where the rain came through
Before we knew it, autumn had grown deep

[Verse 2]
At dawn we fed the fire with wood
The iron pot would rattle first
Beyond the mud wall, from the chimney
Blue smoke rose up straight and tall

[Pre-Chorus]
Mother was in the kitchen
Father paced around the yard
I sat on the worn-down threshold
Watching the whole day go by

[Chorus]
The thatched house, that house
That house that is no more
The smoky smell of straw at evening
The sound of rice boiling in the pot
The thatched house, that house
Still standing inside me

[Verse 3]
Under the low eaves, an old swallow nest
Every spring they never forgot
Five chicks with yellow mouths open wide
The mother flew in and out all day

[Bridge]
Even swallows find their way home
But those who left forget to return
One bundle of straw could hold up a home
At the fallen mud wall, I do not close my eyes
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
The way back is inside me

[Chorus]
The thatched house, that house
That house that is no more
The smoky smell of straw at evening
The sound of rice boiling in the pot
The thatched house, that house
Still standing inside me

[Outro]
Where the smoke rose straight
That house
```
