Chinese-influenced historical ballad, resonant heavy low string drone with steady war drum, weathered and powerful Korean male vocal carrying quiet defiance, gradual building epic intensity, traditional percussion accents, dignified and unbowed, no youthful bravado, 85bpm, triumphant but restrained ending, Korean only
