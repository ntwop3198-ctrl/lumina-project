## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
Every year when December came
Hanging up in every home
A thin little paper almanac
Poor Richard was its name

[Verse 2]
Weather and the phases of the moon
In between the sunrise times
Short and tasty proverbs
Slyly tucked inside

[Pre-Chorus]
Franklin was the writer
Poor Richard was the name

[Chorus]
Early to bed and early to rise
Makes you healthy, wealthy and wise
A small leak left alone
Will sink a great ship
Turning page by page
Poor Richard Almanack

[Interlude]

[Verse 3]
Fish and visitors
Stink after three days
Have you somewhat to do tomorrow
Do it today, he said

[Verse 4]
The first year, all in fun
For his rival almanac man
He foretold the parting day
And when Leeds said he was alive, he held his ground

[Pre-Chorus]
Lost time
Is never found again

[Chorus]
Early to bed and early to rise
Makes you healthy, wealthy and wise
A small leak left alone
Will sink a great ship
Turning page by page
Poor Richard Almanack

[Bridge]
An old man at the auction strung the proverbs into a speech
The crowd all cheered, then went and spent their coins
Saving every penny, every candle end
Not shying from the hard days
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
The wisdom of the old wall almanac stays beside us still

[Final Chorus]
Early to bed and early to rise
Makes you healthy, wealthy and wise
A small leak left alone
Will sink a great ship
Turning page by page
Poor Richard Almanack

[Outro]
Poor Richard
One page rustles
Turning over

[End]
```
