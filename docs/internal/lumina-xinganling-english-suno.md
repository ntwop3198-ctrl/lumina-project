## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Guqin]
[Dizi]
[Spoken]
As far as the eye can see, the Khingan forest sea, birch and pine winds wrap the clouds
Greater and Lesser Khingan run ten thousand li, one green range lights the hills

[Verse 1]
Khingan stands at the northern edge
A sea of forest meets the green ridges
Birch and pine roll in green waves
The old road wind rests on the hills

[Pre-Chorus]
Deep woods reach out to the grassland
A thousand li of forest holds the mist
Deer call through the quiet valleys
A strong wind stirs within the heart

[Chorus]
Khingan, a forest sea so vast
Birch and pine winds touching the sky
Content in green, standing in the snow
A thousand hills, year after year

[Verse 2]
Greater and Lesser Khingan joined as one
A small campfire lights the night
Deep in the woods we track the deer
An old legend passed from age to age

[Pre-Chorus]
Deep woods reach out to the grassland
A thousand li of forest holds the mist
Only the steadfast travel far
A wide spirit lives on in the heart

[Chorus]
Khingan, a forest sea so vast
Birch and pine winds touching the sky
Content in green, standing in the snow
A thousand hills, year after year

[Bridge]
Silent pine winds, birches in a row
Keeping the forest on our own, never bending
Bowing to tend the woods, honoring the small
Locked in ice and snow, enduring the long winter
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고

[Final Chorus]
Khingan, a name that lasts forever
Silent pine winds through a thousand years
Keeping the first heart through the ages
A thousand hills holding up the sky

[Outro]
Below the Khingan, the forest sea so vast
Birch and pine winds, life goes on
```
