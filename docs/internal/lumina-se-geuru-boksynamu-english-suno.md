## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]
The story tells of a spring garden, peach petals in the air
Three men stood under heaven and made a vow to the earth
Though we were not born on the same day
We hope to go together on the same day, they swore

A poor young boy who once wove straw mats
Found two brothers in that way
A bond that ran deeper than brothers
Walked with him through thirty stormy years

[Verse 2]
He rose to fight when the Yellow Turbans came
Again and again he went down and was driven away
He won Xuzhou and lost it, took shelter in Jingzhou
Long were the years he wandered with nothing

But not even once
Did he lose his heart for people
Each time he went down and stood again
It was because others stayed by his side

[Chorus]
The promise made beneath three peach trees
Became the steady flame that ran through his life
He went down and down and still he rose
He knew to win people is to win the land
And that open heart in the end
Made him the one who smiled last

[Verse 3]
A little thatched hut where the Sleeping Dragon lived
Three times he went there on his own two feet
The tale says on the second trip he walked through snow
Only the third time did they sit face to face

High in rank, yet he made himself low
He bowed with respect before a young scholar
And so the people followed him
And stayed by his side until the end

[Chorus]
The promise made beneath three peach trees
Became the steady flame that ran through his life
He went down and down and still he rose
He knew to win people is to win the land

[Bridge]
In Baidicheng, on a bed where his strength was fading
He said if my son is not enough, the charge is yours to keep
The hands that wove straw mats saw people first
Hunted and losing, he never turned from those years
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
He left behind not power, but trust

[Outro]
The spring of peach blossoms, the road to the hut
The last night in Baidicheng, all have gone by
But the heart he kept open to people
Still stays warm after long years
His name is Liu Bei
```
