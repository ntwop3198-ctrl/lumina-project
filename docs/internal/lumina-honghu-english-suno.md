## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Guqin]
[Dizi]
[Spoken]
On Honghu Lake wave beats on wave, ten li of lotus, fish and rice
Morning sun in the reeds, one fishing song rings for ages

[Verse 1]
Reeds grow tall on the Honghu shore
Laughter rings as fishers pick water chestnuts
Old sails once mirrored in the blue
One legend fragrant through the ages

[Pre-Chorus]
Spring tides stir the land of Jingchu
Fish and rice country, golden grain
A thousand waves, nothing to fear
An upright spirit fills the chest

[Chorus]
Honghu Lake, waves rolling on
A hundred li of lotus scent to the sky
Content to be strong, hidden in the reeds
One fishing song, year after year

[Verse 2]
Lotus leaves to the sky in morning light
Evening boats tread the clear waves
The lake village spirit lives on
One true heart never forgotten

[Pre-Chorus]
Spring tides stir the land of Jingchu
Fish and rice country, rice in bloom
Only the strong go far
Where the heart rests is home

[Chorus]
Honghu Lake, waves rolling on
A hundred li of lotus scent to the sky
Content to be strong, hidden in the reeds
One fishing song, year after year

[Bridge]
Lotus scent in silence, wave upon wave
Standing on our own, we never bend
Bowing to work the lake, honoring the small
Through wind and rain, swallowing the toil
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Green reeds, life goes on

[Final Chorus]
Honghu Lake, a name that lasts forever
Silent blue water holds a thousand dreams
Passed down for ages, keeping the first heart
Wide Honghu waves mirror the sky

[Outro]
Green reeds, lotus scent so long
Wave upon wave, life goes on
This is our home
[Fade Out]
```
