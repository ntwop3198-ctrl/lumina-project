## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
At twelve he boarded a ship
His father said, pass within ten years
Or I will not call you my son
In a strange land he read alone

[Verse 2]
In six years he passed
He held office in a foreign land
One piece of writing made his name
That writing still remains today

[Chorus]
He came home, there was no place
They praised his words, but looked at his birth
They did not call him in, they sent him out
Out to the provinces, again and again
He sent up ten-some proposals for change
They were received, and nothing happened

[Verse 3]
The country was tilting
Taxes went unpaid, the people rose
It was not a thing words could mend
And he was not a man of force

[Bridge]
The name he won in a foreign land
He could not use in his own
He took small county posts without complaint
He watched the falling country, did not look away
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Only when he laid down the brush did he grow still

[Chorus]
He came home, there was no place
They praised his words, but looked at his birth
They did not call him in, they sent him out
Out to the provinces, again and again
He sent up ten-some proposals for change
They were received, and nothing happened

[Verse 4]
He left his post and went into Gayasan
They say he took his family along
When he faded, no one wrote down
Some say he left only his shoes behind

[Outro]
His writing lasted a thousand years
The man who wrote it had no place to use it
The country did not last long after
All that remains is what he wrote
[End]
```
