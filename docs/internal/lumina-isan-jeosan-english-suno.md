## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Erhu]
[Acoustic Guitar]

[Verse 1]
The hills around our village
Never had a name
Every other place had one
Only ours had none
But we never minded
Back hill, front hill, side hill
Say this hill or that hill
And everyone would know

[Pre-Chorus]
It was not that they had no name
That was the name

[Chorus]
This hill, that hill, front hill, back hill
Just point a finger and you are done
Towol Mountain, Kalba Mountain
You had to call them by name
But our hills, you just
Said that hill and that was all
Only folks from far away
Never understood our hills

[Verse 2]
Picking greens up the front hill
Grazing cows on the side hill
Whose house beyond that hill
Whose field below this hill
Those words were all we needed
No map was ever needed
It was all inside our heads
A map we learned on foot

[Pre-Chorus]
Names are only needed
When there is much to tell apart

[Chorus]
This hill, that hill, front hill, back hill
Four were plenty for our village
Not hills we knew by calling
Hills we knew by living
Nobody gave them names
No book ever wrote them down
They were just there
So that is what we called them

[Verse 3]
The back hill later on
Got a name of its own
A lime factory moved in
And they called it Lime Hill
They dug and dug again
Till the hill was hollowed out
And from that day on
It was not just the back hill

[Bridge]
[Guitar Only]
The hill stayed as it was
But when people put things there, names came
Little hills we knew with just a finger
The hollowed place, I do not look away
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
The factory went, the name remained

[Final Chorus]
This hill, that hill, front hill, back hill
No reason now to call them
The ones who spoke those words
Many are not with us now
But when I close my eyes
They are all still there
Without a single name
Hills we all knew

[Outro]
[Erhu]
[Guitar Single Note]
That hill
Just those words
And we all knew
```
