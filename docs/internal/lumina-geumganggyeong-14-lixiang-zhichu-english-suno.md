## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Subhuti heard this sutra
grasped its depth and wept
How rare, World-Honored One
since my wisdom eye opened I have never heard such words

[Verse 2]
Leave every form behind
and that is called the Buddha
who hears this and is not startled
not afraid, is rare and precious

[Pre-Chorus]
Give while clinging and you walk into the dark
give without clinging, a sunlit morning

[Chorus]
Beyond all forms is the heart's own ground
the place where I set down the self
not startled and not trembling
one clear tear running down
Beyond all forms is the heart's own ground
that place is called the Buddha

[Verse 3]
Someone who heard unjust words
and never flushed with anger
without a thought of holding back
just smiled and walked on by

[Pre-Chorus]
Give while clinging and you walk into the dark
give without clinging, a sunlit morning

[Chorus]
Beyond all forms is the heart's own ground
the place where I set down the self
not startled and not trembling
one clear tear running down
Beyond all forms is the heart's own ground
that place is called the Buddha

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
in the dark I could see nothing at all
eyes open, sun rising, every color there
Give while clinging and you walk into the dark
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
that place is called the Buddha

[Chorus]
Beyond all forms is the heart's own ground
the place where I set down the self
not startled and not trembling
one clear tear running down
Beyond all forms is the heart's own ground
that place is called the Buddha

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Free of every form, this is called Buddha
unstartled, unafraid, unshaken, rare indeed
```
