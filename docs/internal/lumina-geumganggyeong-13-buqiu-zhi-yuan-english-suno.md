## Title

```
The Wish That Does Not Wish
```

부제: Diamond Sutra Chapter 13, Receiving and Holding as the Dharma  
문서 한글: **바라지 않는 바람이 참 바람이다** · 보컬 **남성** · 따뜻하고 차분하게

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar, light breeze]

[Verse 1]
What shall this sutra be called
Call it Diamond Prajna Paramita
by wisdom hard as diamond
the way across to the other shore

[Verse 2]
Yet the Prajna Paramita the Buddha speaks
is not Prajna Paramita
dust is not dust
the world is not the world

[Pre-Chorus]
He gives it a name
and takes it right back

[Chorus]
The wish that wishes nothing is the true wish
strain to cross and the river grows wide
where you simply let go
the far shore is already here
The wish that wishes nothing is the true wish
Paramita, paramita

[Verse 3]
The hill where as a child I flew my kite
pull the string to make it soar, it dives to the ground
let the string out slowly
it rides the wind to the edge of the sky

[Pre-Chorus]
He gives it a name
and takes it right back

[Chorus]
The wish that wishes nothing is the true wish
strain to cross and the river grows wide
where you simply let go
the far shore is already here
The wish that wishes nothing is the true wish
Paramita, paramita

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
I set down what I wished for and the wind begins to blow
empty hands, one kite high in the sky
He gives it a name
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
Paramita, paramita

[Chorus]
The wish that wishes nothing is the true wish
strain to cross and the river grows wide
where you simply let go
the far shore is already here
The wish that wishes nothing is the true wish
Paramita, paramita

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
The Buddha speaks of Prajna Paramita
it is not Prajna Paramita
```
