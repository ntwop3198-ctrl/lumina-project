## ZH · 过了河 就放下筏

```
过了河 就放下筏
```

```
Mandarin Chinese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, gentle flowing river feel, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Mandarin vocal, 72 BPM, Mandarin Chinese only
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

```

[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]

很久以后的人们
听到这些话

也会有人一念净信
他早已在无数佛前
种下了善根

[Pre-Chorus]
我说的法
就像一只筏

[Chorus]
过了河 就把筏放下

难道要背着上山吗
过了河 就放下吧
过了河 就把筏放下
法都要舍 还抓什么

[Verse 3]
小时候学骑自行车
后面扶着我的那只手

我已经一个人在骑

[Pre-Chorus]
我说的法
就像一只筏

[Chorus]
过了河 就把筏放下
即使是恩重的筏
难道要背着上山吗

[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
越是抓紧 越在河中央
松开的手前 就是彼岸

진공묘유
求小求苦求己, 不向外求
过了河 就放下吧

过了河 就把筏放下
即使是恩重的筏
难道要背着上山吗
过了河 就放下吧

法尚应舍 何况非法
```

## EN · Once Across, Leave the Raft

```
Once Across, Leave the Raft
```

```
English acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, gentle flowing river feel, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male English vocal, 72 BPM, English only
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Subhuti asked once more
Will people far in the future
Hearing these words
Truly believe

[Verse 2]
Do not say such a thing
Even five hundred years from now
There will be one who believes with a clear heart
One who beside countless Buddhas
Has already planted the seed

[Pre-Chorus]
My teaching
Is like a raft

[Chorus]
Once across the river, leave the raft
Even a raft you are grateful for
Will you carry it on your back up the mountain
Once across, set it down
Once across the river, leave the raft
If even the Dharma is let go, what is left to hold

[Verse 3]
When I was small, learning to ride a bike
A hand held on from behind
Somewhere along the way it let go
And I was riding on my own

[Pre-Chorus]
My teaching
Is like a raft

[Chorus]
Once across the river, leave the raft
Even a raft you are grateful for
Will you carry it on your back up the mountain
Once across, set it down
Once across the river, leave the raft
If even the Dharma is let go, what is left to hold

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
The tighter you hold, the deeper in the river
Where the hand lets go, the far shore appears
My teaching
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
Once across, set it down

[Chorus]
Once across the river, leave the raft
Even a raft you are grateful for
Will you carry it on your back up the mountain
Once across, set it down
Once across the river, leave the raft
If even the Dharma is let go, what is left to hold

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Know that my teaching is like a raft
Even the Dharma must be let go, how much more what is not Dharma
```
