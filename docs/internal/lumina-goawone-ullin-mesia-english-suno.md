## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
The curtain fell on the glittering London stage
Debt and a failing body, alone in the night
When all of them said his songs were through
Notes broke the darkness and fell like lightning

[Pre-Chorus]
Twenty-four days and nights he poured it out in tears
The story says he saw all heaven open wide

[Chorus]
Hallelujah, a song that flows to the lowest place
It strokes the thin cheeks of the cold and hungry young
They say the king himself rose from his seat
But the place this song was meant to reach
Was a small orphan room, by the angels left alone

[Verse 2]
He opened the doors of the Foundling Hospital chapel
Year after year at the keys he gave his whole heart
Every ticket sold became a child bread
And each time the singing spread a frozen room grew warm

[Pre-Chorus]
The world applause may scatter like the wind
The smiles of children holding hands stay long

[Chorus]
Hallelujah, a song that flows to the lowest place
It strokes the thin cheeks of the cold and hungry young
They say the king himself rose from his seat
But the place this song was meant to reach
Was a small orphan room, by the angels left alone

[Interlude]

[Bridge]
The last will he left as he faded
Said music is love that holds people up
One set of the score was gift enough
He did not turn from his dimming eyes
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Beside the smallest ones he sings hallelujah

[Final Chorus]
Hallelujah, a mercy ringing on through the years
Faded eyes kept watch on the little flowers
The heavenly melody not for palaces high
Was spent to lift up small and humble lives
A saving harmony still ringing now

[Outro]
Messiah held in the children warm embrace
Hallelujah, hallelujah

[End]
```
