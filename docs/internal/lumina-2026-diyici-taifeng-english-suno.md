## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
The first typhoon of twenty twenty six
Came a little sharper than the years before
Sheet iron at the window clicked all night
I did not turn the light off, I did not sleep
[Verse 2]
The day you left, the wind was just like this
Clothes from the balcony blown across the ground
I went down and picked them up one by one
Only in my arms I saw that you had already gone
[Chorus]
The typhoon passed and took everything
Only wet leaves and water on the ground
I stand at the empty street corner
The wind has stopped, yet I cannot stand steady
[Verse 3]
This city dried out the next day
The big sun rose as usual
As if nothing had happened
Only I know some things are gone
[Bridge]
Snow can pile up, wind only blows through
What piles up can wait to melt
What blows through is truly gone
I do not turn from the small empty place
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Only today I understand
[Chorus]
The typhoon passed and took everything
Only wet leaves and water on the ground
I stand at the empty street corner
The wind has stopped, yet I cannot stand steady
[Outro]
The first typhoon of this year
It was autumn of twenty twenty six
I have kept it
[Fade Out]
```
