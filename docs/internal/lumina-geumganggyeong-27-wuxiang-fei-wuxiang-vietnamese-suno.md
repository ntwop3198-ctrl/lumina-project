## Style

```
Vietnamese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, quiet yet quietly hopeful like winter turning to spring, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Vietnamese vocal, 70 BPM, Vietnamese only
```
