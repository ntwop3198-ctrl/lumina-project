## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Wherever this sutra is spoken
even just four lines of it
that place, by heaven and by all people,
is honored like a stupa, like a shrine

[Verse 2]
How much more the one who holds it whole
who reads it and recites it
has gained the rarest Dharma
where this sutra is, the Buddha is

[Pre-Chorus]
A stupa is not built of stone
a temple is not made of tile

[Chorus]
That place is the heart's own ground
where a single line is passed along
a kitchen or a market square
that place becomes a stupa
That place is the heart's own ground
the Buddha is right there

[Verse 3]
Before the kitchen fire at dawn
Grandma softly chants the sutra
the soot-dark hearth
shone brighter than the temple hall

[Pre-Chorus]
A stupa is not built of stone
a temple is not made of tile

[Chorus]
That place is the heart's own ground
where a single line is passed along
a kitchen or a market square
that place becomes a stupa
That place is the heart's own ground
the Buddha is right there

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
feet that wandered far to find a temple
I come home and sit right down, this is the temple hall
A stupa is not built of stone
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
the Buddha is right there

[Chorus]
That place is the heart's own ground
where a single line is passed along
a kitchen or a market square
that place becomes a stupa
That place is the heart's own ground
the Buddha is right there

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Wherever this sutra is spoken, even four lines
where this sutra is, there the Buddha is
```
