## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
If someone says the Tathagata comes
if someone says the Tathagata goes
sits down or lies to rest
they have not grasped my meaning

[Verse 2]
[Quiet and reflective, minimal nylon guitar picking]
That morning he went into the city
came back, washed his feet and sat
and all that coming and going
was not coming and going

[Pre-Chorus]
Coming from nowhere
going nowhere at all

[Chorus]
The Tathagata neither comes nor goes
and so he is called the Tathagata
as if coming, as if going
always here
The Tathagata neither comes nor goes
open your eyes, he's there

[Verse 3]
[Warm nostalgic tone, gentle pause before the last line]
Walking home at night when I was small
the moon would follow me
I stopped and looked up at the sky
the moon was right where it had been

[Pre-Chorus]
Coming from nowhere
going nowhere at all

[Chorus]
The Tathagata neither comes nor goes
and so he is called the Tathagata
as if coming, as if going
always here
The Tathagata neither comes nor goes
open your eyes, he's there

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
a thousand rivers hold a thousand moons
one moon in the sky
Coming from nowhere
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
open your eyes, he's there

[Chorus]
The Tathagata neither comes nor goes
and so he is called the Tathagata
as if coming, as if going
always here
The Tathagata neither comes nor goes
open your eyes, he's there

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
The Tathagata comes from nowhere and goes nowhere
and so is called the Tathagata
```
