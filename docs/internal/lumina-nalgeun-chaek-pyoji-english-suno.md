## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
A printer, twenty-two years old
Took up his pen half in jest
And wrote the words for his own grave
Long before he would need them

[Verse 2]
The body of B. Franklin, Printer
Its contents torn out
Stript of its lettering and gilding
Like the cover of an old book

[Pre-Chorus]
Lies here
Food for worms though it may be

[Chorus]
The cover of an old book
But the work shall not be lost
Revised and corrected by the Author
In a new and more elegant edition
It will appear once more
Franklin, the printer

[Interlude]

[Verse 3]
Years went by and his good name
Traveled far and wide
Yet the first line of his will said
I, Benjamin Franklin, printer

[Verse 4]
But on the grave where he sleeps
Not that witty verse at all
Side by side with his wife he rests
Two lines of names in stone

[Pre-Chorus]
And still upon that stone today
A penny saved is laid

[Chorus]
The cover of an old book
But the work shall not be lost
Revised and corrected by the Author
In a new and more elegant edition
It will appear once more
Franklin, the printer

[Bridge]
The fortune that he left behind
He gave young craftsmen to begin
Two lines of names, one penny, were enough
He laughed aloud as flesh returned to dust
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
The cover turns to earth, the words wake in a new edition

[Final Chorus]
The cover of an old book
But the work shall not be lost
Revised and corrected by the Author
In a new and more elegant edition
It will appear once more
Franklin, the printer

[Outro]
The cover of an old book
In a new and more elegant edition
Franklin, the printer

[End]
```
