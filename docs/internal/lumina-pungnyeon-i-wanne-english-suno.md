## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[kkwaenggwari janggu taepyeongso]
얼쑤
The grain is ripening across the fields
But in the world of men necks stand stiff and proud

[Verse 1]
Every golden stalk of rice bows its head politely
When the autumn wind comes blowing it dances humbly
Oh my but look around the world of men what a sight
Hollow on the inside yet their chins are held up high

[Verse 2]
The ripe persimmon lowers its branch and offers its hand
The filling grain keeps its eyes on the earth as it grows
Seeing folks who know a little strut around so proud
Empty carts rattle loudest ain't that saying true

[Pre-Chorus]
Empty husks with nothing in them make the loudest noise
Full grain ripens quietly without a single boast
Keep your head up stiff too long and frost will find you first
Stubborn more than any grain and not ashamed at all

[Chorus]
The harvest has come the autumn has come
The riper the grain the lower it bows
Oh life so empty yet so stiff in the neck
Full grain is true grain a ripened heart makes a true man
Look at the harvest field and learn how to bow

[Verse 3]
Shiny on the outside only chaff within that kind of life
One gust of autumn wind and whoosh it blows away
Like a rice stalk full of grain that weathered wind and rain
Let's fill ourselves in silence bow our heads and live

[Bridge]
구소구고구기

진공묘유
```
