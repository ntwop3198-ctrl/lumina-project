## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
On every Mount Sumeru in all the worlds
pile treasure just as high
and give it all away
a great blessing you might say

[Verse 2]
Yet one who holds four lines of this sutra
reads them and tells them to another
measured against that blessing
the treasure is not one in a hundred

[Pre-Chorus]
No counting can measure it
no comparison can reach it

[Chorus]
Greater than Mount Sumeru
one line of four-line wisdom
treasure grows heavier the more you pile
wisdom grows brighter the more you share
Greater than Mount Sumeru
a single lamp is greater than the mountain

[Verse 3]
[Quiet and dark, sudden warmth on last line]
Walk into a storehouse full of treasure
when the light goes out it's pitch dark
but the moment you strike a match
you can see it all

[Pre-Chorus]
No counting can measure it
no comparison can reach it

[Chorus]
Greater than Mount Sumeru
one line of four-line wisdom
treasure grows heavier the more you pile
wisdom grows brighter the more you share
Greater than Mount Sumeru
a single lamp is greater than the mountain

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
the mountains we pile will one day fall
one small light that never goes out
No counting can measure it
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
a single lamp is greater than the mountain

[Chorus]
Greater than Mount Sumeru
one line of four-line wisdom
treasure grows heavier the more you pile
wisdom grows brighter the more you share
Greater than Mount Sumeru
a single lamp is greater than the mountain

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Against that merit, not one part in a hundred
no count, no comparison can reach it
```
