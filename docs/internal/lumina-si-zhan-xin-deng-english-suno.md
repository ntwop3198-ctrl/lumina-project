## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
The human heart is like a pool of water
One passing breeze and wild waves rise
Because love that runs deep turns to deep resentment
It stirs the mind and brings on sorrow

[Verse 2]
A bodhi tree before the old temple steps
Leaves fall without a sound, turning one by one
The old monk smiled and softly said
Let this feeling settle back in stillness

[Pre-Chorus]
No need to force your feelings away
Just light a lamp within your heart

[Chorus]
The first lamp, reason clears the muddy water
The second lamp, compassion warms the cold heart
The third lamp, by courtesy know when to step and yield
The fourth lamp, follow virtue to light the road ahead
Four lamps of the heart, shining on and on
The one who carries me across is me

[Interlude]

[Verse 3]
One sentence once lodged in my heart
I carried in my chest for decades
Looking back, I see it wasn't their fault
It was me who never changed

[Verse 4]
Decades of years we walked together
Kindness and grudges, all ties from lives before
Letting go of hands is not giving in
It lets us both walk farther on

[Pre-Chorus]
Hate burns the heart, love wounds so easily
Only the lamplight breaks through the haze

[Chorus]
The first lamp, reason clears the muddy water
The second lamp, compassion warms the cold heart
The third lamp, by courtesy know when to step and yield
The fourth lamp, follow virtue to light the road ahead
Four lamps of the heart, shining on and on
The one who carries me across is me

[Bridge]
Beneath the bodhi tree, a clear breeze passes
Leaves fall as they will, the heart stays calm
A cup of clear tea, a scroll of ink
Pain in my chest, I walk on open and true
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
진공묘유, the heart lamp ever bright

[Final Chorus]
The first lamp, reason clears the muddy water
The second lamp, compassion warms the cold heart
The third lamp, by courtesy know when to step and yield
The fourth lamp, follow virtue to light the road ahead
Four lamps of the heart, shining on and on
The one who carries me across is me

[Outro]
Four lamps of the heart
Shine through the ford of confusion
And carry me back home myself
```
