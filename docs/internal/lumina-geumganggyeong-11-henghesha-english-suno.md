## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
If every grain of Ganges sand
became a Ganges of its own
then all the sand in all those rivers
would it be many

[Verse 2]
Very many, World-Honored One
those rivers alone are beyond count
how much more their sand
and the Buddha spoke once more

[Pre-Chorus]
Fill as many worlds as those sands with treasure
and give it all away

[Chorus]
Sands of the Ganges
more than all those treasures
keep one verse of four short lines
and share it, the blessing is greater
Sands of the Ganges
the uncounted blessing is greater

[Verse 3]
A teacher's word when I was small
you can do it
that single line all my life
has lifted me to my feet

[Pre-Chorus]
Fill as many worlds as those sands with treasure
and give it all away

[Chorus]
Sands of the Ganges
more than all those treasures
keep one verse of four short lines
and share it, the blessing is greater
Sands of the Ganges
the uncounted blessing is greater

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
counting sand and counting on, it never ends
one word carries us across the river
Fill as many worlds as those sands with treasure
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
the uncounted blessing is greater

[Chorus]
Sands of the Ganges
more than all those treasures
keep one verse of four short lines
and share it, the blessing is greater
Sands of the Ganges
the uncounted blessing is greater

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Hold four lines and tell them to another
this merit surpasses the merit before
```
