## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
Word came the expert convoy would arrive
Overnight the yard was laid with young wheat
All for the shine of a name on the list
In one night the green burst open
[Verse 2]
On the dry slope behind the hill
A coat of blue green oil paint was sprayed
From far away it looked like spring wind
Up close it stained the hands with paint
[Pre-Chorus]
I carried a bucket, held the rag tight
Wiped every corner again and again
[Chorus]
Playground wheat, green then gone in a day
The convoy comes and goes like a dream
One borrowed day of false spring light
Polish it bright, still no one looks
Tomorrow the sun rises on an empty yard
[Interlude]
[Verse 3]
A rumbling tractor rolls in again
Young wheat turned under into clods
At dusk a heavy rain comes down
Green runs down the hillside
[Verse 4]
Blisters on the palm turned to callus
The washed white rag tossed aside
The school later made the list as wished
Who still remembers the wheat field of that day
[Pre-Chorus]
Brush the face as bright as you like
One hard rain and the color lifts
[Chorus]
Playground wheat, green then gone in a day
The convoy comes and goes like a dream
One borrowed day of false spring light
Polish it bright, still no one looks
Tomorrow the sun rises on an empty yard
[Bridge]
Why raise a spring wind in the night
Why smear the peaks with paint
Sweep the small yard before the door
Spring will come visiting on its own
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
True green grows slow
[Final Chorus]
Playground wheat, green then gone in a day
The convoy comes and goes like a dream
One borrowed day of false spring light
Polish it bright, still no one looks
Tomorrow the sun rises on an empty yard
[Outro]
Playground wheat
Green then gone in a day
[Fade Out]
```
