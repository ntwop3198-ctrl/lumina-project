[Intro]
[Train Whistle]

Those hands knocking on the train window, those days kept in my heart, I miss them

In a cramped dorm room under one quilt
Even one pot of noodles made us laugh
Different words, a strange land
But you patted me on the back

[Verse 2]

Everyone lay down to sleep, I was on the floor
Jumping, stomping, yelling at the top of my lungs

[Pre-Chorus]
That night even Big Brother kept quiet
Then Lao Er said, come on, that is enough

Arm in arm we went to breakfast, that was us

[Chorus]
I miss those days, I miss that room
Lao Er, Lao Si, are you doing well
On the Changchun platform, knocking on the train
Years go by, it still pounds in my chest
I miss you, my friends, I miss those days

[Verse 3]
Lao Er became a big professor in Beijing
At Mid-Autumn in Changchun, his hand was warm
In a cold world where even family turn away
You, no kin of mine, were my true brothers

The ruckus of that night is a laugh now
