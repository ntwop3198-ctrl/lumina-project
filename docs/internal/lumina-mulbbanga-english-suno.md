## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Stream Sounds]

[Verse 1]
By the clear stream in Yanbian
I jumped in barefoot with a splash
Just to catch one little sand fish
My trouser legs got soaking wet

[Verse 2]
Grandma calling me home to eat
Her voice came riding on the sunset
I played like I never heard a thing
Staring into water between the stones

[Pre-Chorus]
Cupping both my palms so still
Creeping closer, slow and quiet
It wriggled down into the sand
Flicked its tail just to tease me

[Chorus]
Little sand fish, where did you hide
Every time I find you, you run
Every time I catch you, you slip away
Still we laughed all day back then
Little sand fish, I miss you so
That stream, those days, still in my eyes

[Verse 3]
Through hard and stormy years
Now my hair has turned to white
But the cool water brushing my fingers
And your little flash are clear as day

[Bridge]
Looking back down the winding road
One little fish made my whole day
Lost it, soaked, I reached in again
That stream still runs inside me
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
The clear child I was is standing there

[Chorus]
Little sand fish, where did you hide
Every time I find you, you run
Every time I catch you, you slip away
Still we laughed all day back then
Little sand fish, I miss you so
That stream, those days, still in my eyes

[Outro]
Years drifting far along the water
That little stream in Yanbian
Home inside my heart
I miss it still today
[Fade Out]
```
