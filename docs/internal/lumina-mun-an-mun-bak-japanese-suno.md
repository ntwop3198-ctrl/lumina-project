```
Polka, warm male vocal with playful phrasing, dry mono studio mix, accordion leading over oom-pah tuba, bright banjo and crisp handclaps, brisk bouncing pulse with a half-time bridge and a lively finish, Japanese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```
