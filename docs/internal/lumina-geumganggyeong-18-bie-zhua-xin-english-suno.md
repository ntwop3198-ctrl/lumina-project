## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Does the Tathagata have the eye of flesh
He does, World-Honored One
the heavenly eye, the wisdom eye, the Dharma eye
and the Buddha eye as well

[Verse 2]
As many worlds as Ganges sands
and all the minds of beings in those worlds
the Tathagata knows them all
for all those minds are not mind

[Pre-Chorus]
Past mind, present mind, future mind
none of them can be held

[Chorus]
Don't grab the mind, it can't be held
can your hand hold running water
while you reach to catch this mind
it's already the mind gone by
Don't grab the mind, it can't be held
just let it flow

[Verse 3]
[Playful storytelling]
A monk who carried the Diamond Sutra on his back
asked an old woman selling cakes for a midday snack
she said past, present, future mind cannot be held
so which mind will your snack refresh, master
the monk could not say a word
and turned away without a cake

[Pre-Chorus]
Past mind, present mind, future mind
none of them can be held

[Chorus]
Don't grab the mind, it can't be held
can your hand hold running water
while you reach to catch this mind
it's already the mind gone by
Don't grab the mind, it can't be held
just let it flow

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
I open my clenched fist, an empty hand
in the empty hand, a piece of cake
Past mind, present mind, future mind
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
just let it flow

[Chorus]
Don't grab the mind, it can't be held
can your hand hold running water
while you reach to catch this mind
it's already the mind gone by
Don't grab the mind, it can't be held
just let it flow

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Past mind cannot be grasped, present mind cannot be grasped
future mind cannot be grasped
```
