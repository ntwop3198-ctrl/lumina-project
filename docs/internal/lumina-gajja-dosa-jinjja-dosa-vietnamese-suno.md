## Style

```
Korean satirical folk pop with a swaggering minyo groove, a greedy fake mystic selling talismans and fear versus a humble real monk sweeping his temple yard, sly theatrical male vocal switching between a pompous fake and a plain gentle real voice, janggu, buk, kkwaenggwari, moktak wood block, gayageum, singing bowl, tuba-like low brass, small mountain temple yard with broom sweeping ambience, strutting comic rhythm turning calm in the bridge, witty biting mood, 98 bpm, Vietnamese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```
