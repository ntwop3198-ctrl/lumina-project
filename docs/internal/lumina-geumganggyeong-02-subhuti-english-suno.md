## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Moktak and nylon guitar, light]

[Verse 1]
He had eaten, washed his feet
And had only just sat down
Among twelve hundred and fifty people
Of all people, one stood up

[Verse 2]
Right shoulder bared
Right knee on the ground
Palms together, he asked
How rare, O World Honored One

[Pre-Chorus]
Everyone thought inside
Why now, of all times

[Chorus]
Subhuti cannot read the room
Just as He would rest, he asks again
Where should the mind abide
How can this mind be tamed
Subhuti cannot read the room
With one question the sutra opened

[Verse 3]
The Buddha smiled and said
Well asked, well asked
Listen closely, I will tell you
Yes, I long to hear

[Chorus]
Subhuti cannot read the room
Just as He would rest, he asks again
Where should the mind abide
How can this mind be tamed
Subhuti cannot read the room
With one question the sutra opened

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
It was not that he could not read the room
He knew that we who sat behind
Would never dare to ask
So he stood up in our place

[Chorus]
[Tender and warm]
Subhuti cannot read the room
Just as He would rest, he asks again
Where should the mind abide
How can this mind be tamed
Subhuti cannot read the room
With one question the sutra opened

[Outro]
[Slowing down]
Subhuti cannot read the room
Thank you, Subhuti
```
