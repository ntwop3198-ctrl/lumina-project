## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Low geomungo, wind and Gangjin sea residual]

[Verse 1]
Gangjin sea wind cut to the core
Still he never put the brush down
Eighteen years in exile, the world turned away
Over muddy water, a blue flower bloomed
He never blamed the sky
The deeper the hardship, the deeper the roots
Dasan knew it well

[Verse 2]
Every word of Mokmin Simseo, the people's tears
A weary brush wove order for this land
Those who left him, he never blamed
Five hundred volumes answered a broken age
Power and fame all fade away
His brush still changed the world

[Chorus]
Hardship is the teacher, pain is the way
At the end of the ache, the truth opens
Stop blaming others, seek only yourself
Dasan's lamp still burns inside us

[Bridge]
Not eighteen years lost, eighteen years gained
The true road was always within
See the essence in the small things
Face the pain, do not turn away
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Exile was his teacher, hardship his garden

[Verse 3]
Pine wind at Dasan Chodang still sways
On each dewy inkstone his spirit stays
We all carry an exile of our own
Break the shell of pain and fully bloom

[Final Chorus]
Hardship is the teacher, pain is the way
At the end of the ache, the truth opens
Stop blaming others, seek only yourself
Dasan's lamp still burns inside us

[Outro]
Like the deep silence of the Gangjin sea
Dew gathers at Dasan Chodang
Let us bloom again
[Fade out]
[End]
```
