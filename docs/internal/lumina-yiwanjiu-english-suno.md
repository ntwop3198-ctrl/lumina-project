## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro: erhu and faint tavern noise]

[Verse 1: Voice A, the one who lost]
What brings you to a place like this
Don't you have that half acre to tend
There's nothing much on my table
Just one bowl of wine don't think it poor

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 2: Voice B, the one who kept]
My half acre's nothing precious either
I just never dared to let it go
Back then you were far bolder than me
Luck just didn't stand on your side

[Pre-Chorus: Call and Response]
[Voice A] It's not that I never thought of turning back
[Voice B] Turning back is never that easy

[Chorus: Duet Harmony]
One bowl of wine half for you half for me
I won't tell you to quit won't laugh at how hard it's been
This world is full of
People who know better and still can't get out
One bowl of wine half for you half for me
Tomorrow's trouble can wait for tomorrow
Tonight just don't drink alone
With someone beside you it isn't over

[Verse 3: Voice B]
I won't tell you what you ought to do
Those aren't words I can say

[Verse 3: Voice A]
Not saying them is how I know you understand
Those who talk have never been stuck at this hill

[Bridge: instruments drop to harmonica and two voices]
[Voice A] That year you asked me to come along with you
[Voice B] And you said you'd go and try your luck
[Voice A] I still haven't made it through
[Voice B] But you still haven't fallen down
[Duet Harmony] Then let it be let it be
Finish this bowl and it's nearly dawn

[Final Chorus: erhu enters, fuller]
One bowl of wine half for you half for me
I won't tell you to quit won't laugh at how hard it's been
This world is full of
People who know better and still can't get out
One bowl of wine half for you half for me
Tomorrow's trouble can wait for tomorrow
Tonight just don't drink alone
With someone beside you it isn't over

[Outro: erhu fading, two sets of footsteps]
[Voice B] Let's go
[Voice A] Let's go
[Duet Harmony] There's more work tomorrow
```
