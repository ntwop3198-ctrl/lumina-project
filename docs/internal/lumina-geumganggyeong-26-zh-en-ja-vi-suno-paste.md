```
法身无形
```

以声音求我

진공묘유

法身无形

## EN · The Dharma Body Has No Form

```
The Dharma Body Has No Form
```

```
English acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, calm and deep with a touch of warmth, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male English vocal, 70 BPM, English only
```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
[Playful storytelling]
By the thirty-two marks
can the Tathagata be seen
Yes indeed, he can be seen
Subhuti answered in a rush

[Verse 2]
[Amused smile, soft picking]
Then the wheel-turning king
must be the Tathagata too
Subhuti quickly corrected himself
by marks he cannot be seen

[Pre-Chorus]
If you see me in a form
or seek me in a sound

[Chorus]
The Dharma body has no form
seek by form and you see only form
seek by sound and you hear only sound
that road will never see the Tathagata
The Dharma body has no form
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Verse 3]
Chanting the Buddha's name out loud
beating the wooden fish hard and high
if you seek through sound alone
only the sound remains

[Pre-Chorus]
If you see me in a form
or seek me in a sound

[Chorus]
The Dharma body has no form
seek by form and you see only form
seek by sound and you hear only sound
that road will never see the Tathagata
The Dharma body has no form
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
where this song comes to an end
in the stillness, the Tathagata
If you see me in a form
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Chorus]
The Dharma body has no form
seek by form and you see only form
seek by sound and you hear only sound
that road will never see the Tathagata
The Dharma body has no form
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Who sees me by form, who seeks me in sound
walks a wrong path and cannot see the Tathagata
```

## JA · 法身に形はない

法身に形はない

```

```

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, enka, female vocal, choir
```

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
[Playful storytelling]
三十二の相で
如来を見ることができるか
そうです 見ることができます
須菩提はすぐさま答えた

[Verse 2]
[Amused smile, soft picking]
それなら転輪聖王も
如来ということになるな
須菩提はあわてて言い直した
相では見られません

[Pre-Chorus]
姿でわたしを見たり
声でわたしを求めるなら

[Chorus]
法身に形はない
姿で探せば姿だけが見え
声で探せば声だけが聞こえる
その道では如来は見えない
法身に形はない
探すその心が如来

[Verse 3]
念仏を大きく唱え
木魚を高く打ち鳴らしても
声だけで探すなら
その声だけが残る

[Pre-Chorus]
姿でわたしを見たり
声でわたしを求めるなら

[Chorus]
法身に形はない
姿で探せば姿だけが見え
声で探せば声だけが聞こえる
その道では如来は見えない
法身に形はない
探すその心が如来

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
この歌が終わったところ
静けさの中に如来
姿でわたしを見たり
진공묘유
小を求め 苦を求め 己を求め, 外に求めず
探すその心が如来

[Chorus]
法身に形はない
姿で探せば姿だけが見え
声で探せば声だけが聞こえる
その道では如来は見えない
法身に形はない
探すその心が如来

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
若し色を以て我を見 音声を以て我を求むれば
是の人は邪道を行じ 如来を見ること能わず
```
