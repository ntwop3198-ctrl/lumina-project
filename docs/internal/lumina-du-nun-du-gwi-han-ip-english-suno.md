## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Warm fingerstyle acoustic guitar arpeggio, gentle piano melody enters]
Why we were given two eyes and two ears
And only one mouth to speak

[Verse 1]
To look upon the world twice as deep
To listen twice before I speak
Because I know a careless word let fly
Can leave a wound in someone's heart

[Pre-Chorus]
I hold the little things that pass me by
I keep the bitter counsel close inside
And let my restless heart grow still

[Chorus]
With two eyes I see the world whole
With two ears I hear another's pain
My only mouth I open with care
That's the gentle wisdom life gave to me
The more I empty and listen, the warmer I grow
Today again I'll walk on quietly

[Verse 2]
The days I thought my way was always right
One step back, I saw a clumsy greed
The moments I raised my voice to win
Now feel small before a deep silence

[Pre-Chorus]
I hold the little things that pass me by
I keep the bitter counsel close inside
And let my restless heart grow still

[Chorus]
With two eyes I see the world whole
With two ears I hear another's pain
My only mouth I open with care
That's the gentle wisdom life gave to me
The more I empty and listen, the warmer I grow
Today again I'll walk on quietly

[Bridge]
[Guitar strumming builds calmly, cello melody joins]
When I treasure the smallest things
And take the bitter as my teacher
Standing straight within, not blaming others
In the quiet where my words fall away
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
At last the clear light of wisdom blooms

[Final Chorus]
[Full acoustic resonance]
With two eyes I see people warmly
With two ears I hear the silence of the world
With my only mouth I pass on warmth
That's the way of truly growing up
To see more, to listen deeper
To spare my words and leave them sweet

[Outro]
[Fading into solo piano]
Two eyes, two ears, and one mouth
The more I empty, the fuller life's balance
```
