## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
A boy of seventeen
Slipped away from his brother and the press
By boat, on foot and rowing
He reached strange Philadelphia

[Verse 2]
A few coins in his pocket
His tired body worn and slow
The boatmen would not take his fare
Yet he paid them all the same

[Pre-Chorus]
He knocked and opened the bakery door
Threepence worth of bread, he said

[Chorus]
Three puffy rolls
One tucked under each arm
The third one in his mouth
He ambled down the street
A girl stood at her door
And thought he looked absurd

[Interlude]

[Verse 3]
The two rolls left over
To a mother and her child
Who came down the river with him
He gladly gave away

[Verse 4]
Into the Quaker meeting house
On a long wooden bench he sat
In the quiet without a word
He drifted off to sleep

[Pre-Chorus]
The girl who thought he looked absurd
Later became his wife

[Chorus]
Three puffy rolls
One tucked under each arm
The third one in his mouth
He ambled down the street
A girl stood at her door
And thought he looked absurd

[Bridge]
Long after the world knew his name
He wrote that day down clear
An empty pocket, three rolls were plenty
He never ran from that humble start
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
A growing heart that knew no shame

[Final Chorus]
Three puffy rolls
One tucked under each arm
The third one in his mouth
He ambled down the street
A girl stood at her door
And thought he looked absurd

[Outro]
Three puffy rolls
Seventeen
Franklin

[End]
```
