## Style

```
Vietnamese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, light and airy like a breeze over a hill, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Vietnamese vocal, 72 BPM, Vietnamese only
```

---

## Exclude Styles

```
EDM, trap, rap, autotune, metal, screaming, trot, female vocal, choir
```

고급 설정: 기이함 **20%** · 스타일 영향 **70%** · 다양성 보통 · 개인화 끔
