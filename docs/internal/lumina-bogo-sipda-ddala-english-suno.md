## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Warm acoustic guitar]

[Verse 1]
Holding on to the corner of the table
Your little legs trembling as you stood up with a bright smile
You called out daddy
That day feels like yesterday

They say most babies call for mom first
But you called for daddy first
My princess I wonder if you know
You were such a special child

[Chorus]
Daddy's heart swelled right up
Even though twenty years have passed
To daddy it's like yesterday
The memory's still so clear

[Verse 2]
Up at the top of the slide
Like a sun that had just risen
The way you smiled so bright
I can still see it in my eyes

Afraid you might come home late
Daddy waited from a distance
Until I heard your footsteps
I couldn't take my eyes away

[Chorus]
The daddy who said even your messy diapers were sweet
Is still right here
Those tiny toddling steps of yours
Made the whole world shine

[Bridge]
I miss you my daughter
I just watch you from afar
Daddy's love for his princess
Will never change just like the sun

[Final Chorus]
Until the day I hold your hand again
Daddy can wait
I miss you my daughter
I'll always be right here

[Outro]
[Guitar slowly fading]
Daddy can wait
I miss you my daughter
```
