## Style

```
Chinese-influenced historical ballad, heavy low string drone and deep war drum, Vietnamese male vocal, raw and powerful with restrained ferocity, sudden dynamic bursts of intensity, minimal ornamentation, primal and unpolished, dignified undertone beneath the roughness, 84bpm, abrupt and resolute ending, Vietnamese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```
