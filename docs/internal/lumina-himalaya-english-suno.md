## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Guqin]
[Dizi]
[Spoken]
White summits stand above the clouds, the crown of the snowland, ancient and far
Everest rises over heaven and earth, one mighty range touching the sky

[Verse 1]
Everest stands high above the clouds
The snow line runs on through the bitter cold
Ancient glaciers hold heaven and earth
A mighty spine rising to the sky

[Pre-Chorus]
A thousand ice peaks wrap the sky
A gate stands at the top of the snowland
No danger stops a soaring will
Step by step we climb higher

[Chorus]
The Himalayas, crown of the snowland
Everest rising to the sky
Content with the cold, climbing with bowed heads
One line of glaciers, year after year

[Verse 2]
Summit winds sweep the dawn mist
Ice cliffs and sheer walls rise from the range
Climbers cross a thousand dangers
A bold spirit lights the whole world

[Pre-Chorus]
A thousand ice peaks wrap the sky
A gate stands at the top of the snowland
Only those who climb travel far
A fearless heart stands above the clouds

[Chorus]
The Himalayas, crown of the snowland
Everest rising to the sky
Content with the cold, climbing with bowed heads
One line of glaciers, year after year

[Bridge]
Silent glaciers, a rolling sea of clouds
Step by step on our own strength
Bowing as we climb, honoring the small
Through fierce wind and thin air, teeth set, we go on
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
A thousand ice peaks, life goes on

[Final Chorus]
The Himalayas, a name that lasts forever
Silent glaciers keep the ages
Climbing for ages, keeping the first heart
A mighty spine holding up the sky

[Outro]
On the Everest heights, the snowland so vast
A thousand ice peaks, life goes on
[Fade Out]
```
