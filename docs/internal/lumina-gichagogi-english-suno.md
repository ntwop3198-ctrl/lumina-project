## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
Rolling up my trouser legs, around and around
Dipping my feet in the cold pebbled stream
Under sunlight breaking silver on the water
Soft sand tickling between my toes

[Pre-Chorus]
Carefully I would lift a single stone
And the little one would slip into the sand
Cupping both hands, holding my breath
In a blink I scooped it gently up

[Chorus]
Little windows in a row along its side
Where are you running, little train fish
Every window carried dreams of those days
Chugging on with the sound of the shallows

[Verse 2]
An empty soda bottle filled with water clear
I slipped it in and held it to the sun
Rows and rows of tiny black spots
Just like the windows of a running train
You and I looked up and laughed without a sound

[Bridge]
When the sunset turned the sky to red
I let it go in the shallows, said goodbye
One cold drop left on my fingertip
Sad to part, I waved, see you tomorrow
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
That day has never faded through the years

[Chorus]
Little windows in a row along its side
Where are you running, little train fish
Every window carried dreams of those days
Chugging on with the sound of the shallows

[Outro]
Cut through the ripples, run, little train
Back to the creek of those dear days
Into the shallows of my heart
```
