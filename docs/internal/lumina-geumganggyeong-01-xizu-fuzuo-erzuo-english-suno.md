## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Thus have I heard
When it was time to eat
He put on his robe and took his bowl
And walked barefoot into the city

[Verse 2]
Before the great house, before the small
In order, door by door
He took whatever he was given
And came back the way he came

[Pre-Chorus]
Not a word of teaching yet
Not a single word

[Chorus]
He ate and put the bowl away
Washed his feet, spread his seat, and sat
That was all
That was all, and yet
Twelve hundred and fifty people
Sat there holding their breath

[Verse 3]
A thousand years later Linji said
Get dressed, eat your rice, lie down when tired
This morning I too
Get dressed, eat my rice, and wash my feet

[Pre-Chorus]
Not a word of teaching yet
Not a single word

[Chorus]
He ate and put the bowl away
Washed his feet, spread his seat, and sat
That was all
That was all, and yet
Twelve hundred and fifty people
Sat there holding their breath

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
What I was looking for was never far
Beneath washed feet, a single seat
Not a single word
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
That was all

[Chorus]
He ate and put the bowl away
Washed his feet, spread his seat, and sat
That was all
That was all, and yet
Twelve hundred and fifty people
Sat there holding their breath

[Outro]
Thus have I heard
Washed his feet, spread his seat, and sat
```
