## Title

```
Mother-in-Law Meets the Son-in-Law
```

문서 원본: **丈母娘看女婿** · 보컬 **남녀 듀엣** — 장모는 깐깐하게 타박하는 톤, 사위는 넉살 좋게 받아치는 톤, 뒤에 받쳐 주는 코러스

## Style

```
Errenzhuan, roomy vocal-and-instrument bleed, brisk 122 BPM syncopated clapper groove, warm mid-range comedic male-female call-and-response with backing voices and rapid theatrical patter, piercing festive suona lead, rapid banhu fiddle, bamboo clappers, English vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Female]
Oh my, the sun is up on our backsides
Why is the new son-in-law not in the village yet
[Male]
Coming, coming, dear mother-in-law
Your son-in-law brings New Year wishes and a big fish
[Verse 1]
[Female]
Just look at you, what a sight
Riding a Golden Deer bike, the wheels all wobbling
Said you would never come empty-handed
A block of frozen tofu, who do you think you fool
My daughter is pretty as a flower
How did she end up with a raw young lad like you
[Male]
Mother-in-law, please calm down
The back seat is loaded with the good stuff
Two and a half sides of fatty pork
Pure grain liquor in a big blue porcelain jar
For you, I never count the cost
And for your daughter, my heart is hers
[Pre-Chorus]
[Female]
Smooth talker, how many corn harvests this year
[Male]
Barns so full, even the mice need canes to climb in
[Female]
How many coins have you saved in your pocket
[Male]
All to your daughter, I need permission for a pack of tobacco
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
A little hanky flung up to the sky
The more she looks, the more she likes him
Son-in-law toasts mother, cups clink round
Seven little plates and eight bowls
Chicken stewed with mushrooms, steam curling high
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
A family life sweeter than honey
[Interlude]
[Verse 2]
[Female]
You do know how to please, young man
Up on the kang and warm your hands
Caught a free-range chicken out back
Hazel mushrooms and noodles rolling in the pot
If you do not finish three cups today
Do not even think of crossing my door
[Male]
Your word is law, mother
Your son-in-law will drink with you all the way
Left hand, a toast to your strong health
Right hand, a toast to lasting luck
When spring comes we build a brick house
And take you to the city park
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
A little hanky flung up to the sky
The more she looks, the more she likes him
Son-in-law toasts mother, cups clink round
Seven little plates and eight bowls
Chicken stewed with mushrooms, steam curling high
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
A family life sweeter than honey
[Bridge]
[Female]
I do not pick a son-in-law by money
[Male]
Mother looks at whether the heart is good
[Female]
Willing to work and never lazy
[Male]
Good to his wife and kind to the old
[Duet]
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Make your own days good with your own hands
[Outro]
[Female]
Cheeky boy, enough showing off, get on the kang and eat
[Male]
Yes, mother-in-law, let me fill your cup
[Duet]
Ha ha ha ha ha
Good weather and a thriving home
Like sesame flowers, higher every step
[Fade Out]
```
