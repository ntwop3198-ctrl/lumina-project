## Title

```
No High, No Low
```

부제: Diamond Sutra Chapter 23, Neither High Nor Low  
문서 한글: **법은 높고 낮음이 없다** · 보컬 **남성** · 따뜻하고 차분하게

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
This Dharma is equal
with no high and no low
and this is what we name
the supreme awakening

[Verse 2]
With no self and no other
practice every good deed
and there is awakening
yet do not cling to good as good

[Pre-Chorus]
The high mountain and the low field
under one sky

[Chorus]
The Dharma has no high or low
the great temple and the little hermitage
the great one and the small one
sit together in a circle
The Dharma has no high or low
level as still water

[Verse 3]
The first snow fell on tiled roofs
and on the tin roofs too
choosing none, the same for all
it settled down in white

[Pre-Chorus]
The high mountain and the low field
under one sky

[Chorus]
The Dharma has no high or low
the great temple and the little hermitage
the great one and the small one
sit together in a circle
The Dharma has no high or low
level as still water

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
I set down the ladder I climbed to rise
the same earth, the same sky everywhere
The high mountain and the low field
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
level as still water

[Chorus]
The Dharma has no high or low
the great temple and the little hermitage
the great one and the small one
sit together in a circle
The Dharma has no high or low
level as still water

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
This Dharma is equal, without high or low
this is called Anuttara Samyak Sambodhi
```
