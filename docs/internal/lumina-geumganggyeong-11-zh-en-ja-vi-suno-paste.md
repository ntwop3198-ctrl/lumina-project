## ZH · 恒河沙

```
恒河沙
```

```
Mandarin Chinese acoustic meditative folk ballad, soft moktak woodblock, nylon guitar, daegeum, vast and flowing like a great river, guitar drops to solo daegeum on bridge, warm calm male Mandarin vocal, 70 BPM, Mandarin Chinese only
```

```
佛陀又再开口说

你一定能行

진공묘유

恒河的沙
比那许多珍宝
```
