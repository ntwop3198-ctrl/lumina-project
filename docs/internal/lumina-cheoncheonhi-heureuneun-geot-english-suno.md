## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft acoustic guitar, low piano]

[Verse 1]
A pain greater than hard labor is the waiting
Watching something pile up at a speed you cannot see
Again today my heart grows restless, rushing on tomorrow
Even in a life that lacks for nothing, I feel unhappy

[Verse 2]
That unhappiness is not a real lack
It's just a sickness born of endless haste
Turning from today's contentment, clinging to tomorrow
That tomorrow never comes, and when it does, new discontent appears

[Chorus]
But the patient one accepts the pace
```
