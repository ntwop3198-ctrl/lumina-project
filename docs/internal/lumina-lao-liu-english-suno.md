## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Acoustic Guitar]
[Erhu]

[Verse 1]
That year in the dorm, one row of beds, you were sixth
Round face, anyone could see your luck
Even ordering a dish took you forever
We laughed and called you slow as an old ox

[Verse 2]
Last Mid-Autumn, the Changchun moon
Lao Jiu came too, three of us at one table
As the night wore on, the others left
Only you stayed sitting there with me

[Pre-Chorus]
I said I was going, said it three times
You did not move a single step

[Chorus]
Lao Liu, oh Lao Liu
Other people have a sly Lao Liu
My Lao Liu cannot scheme at all
He just stays with me to the end
Lao Liu, oh Lao Liu
My good brother all my life

[Verse 3]
All these years we went our ways
Me in Seoul and you in Changchun
Few calls and fewer words
But one word and the old fire is there

[Pre-Chorus]
I said I was going, said it three times
You did not move a single step

[Chorus]
Lao Liu, oh Lao Liu
Other people have a sly Lao Liu
My Lao Liu cannot scheme at all
He just stays with me to the end
Lao Liu, oh Lao Liu
My good brother all my life

[Bridge]
Those who scheme leave the fastest
Those who hesitate stay to the end
You who took forever to choose a dish
You who stayed when everyone had gone
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Your hesitation was not wanting to leave

[Final Chorus]
Lao Liu, oh Lao Liu
This Mid-Autumn I will not come back
Watch the Changchun moon for me
And drink a glass for me
Lao Liu, oh Lao Liu
My good brother all my life

[Outro]
Lao Liu
The one who stayed to the end
Lao Liu
One word, brother
```
