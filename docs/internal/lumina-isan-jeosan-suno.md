## Style

```
Korean acoustic folk, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, subtle erhu answering lines, upright bass and soft brush snare, gentle strings opening the choruses with a low backing hum, wide space between phrases, warm weathered low male vocal, plain-spoken storytelling, unhurried 86 BPM, verse 3 sparse guitar, bridge nearly bare with guitar harmonics, outro fading on erhu and one guitar note, Korean only
```
