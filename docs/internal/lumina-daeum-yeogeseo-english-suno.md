## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
The moment we sit in the priority seats
My old girl starts and will not stop
Why did you leave your morning soup
Why kick your shoes off anywhere
[Pre-Chorus]
Everyone on the train is staring
I just keep looking out the window
Doors open, she says get off first
I just stay sitting, nice and still
[Chorus]
I got off at the next station
Squatted at the end of a bench
One train, then another
Craning my neck, is she coming
Where did my big talk go
Only my shoulders slumping low
[Verse 2]
Riding alone, is that so great
I ask myself again and again
The seat beside me is so wide
Today I found out for the first time
[Pre-Chorus]
Doors open, a purple scarf
There she stands, arms folded
Not a word, she hands me her bag
And I take it with both hands
[Chorus]
I got off at the next station
Squatted at the end of a bench
One train, then another
Craning my neck, is she coming
Where did my big talk go
Only my shoulders slumping low
[Bridge]
Forty years of fights, still the same road home
Nagging only sounds when someone is there to hear
The empty seat beside me, wide and cold
Bickering side by side is the taste of life
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
I take the hand of the one beside me
[Interlude]
[Outro]
Tonight the soup will be salty
But we will eat it together
At the next station I waited
Waited on her way to me
Ha ha
[Fade Out]
```
