## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
[Playful storytelling]
By the thirty-two marks
can the Tathagata be seen
Yes indeed, he can be seen
Subhuti answered in a rush

[Verse 2]
[Amused smile, soft picking]
Then the wheel-turning king
must be the Tathagata too
Subhuti quickly corrected himself
by marks he cannot be seen

[Pre-Chorus]
If you see me in a form
or seek me in a sound

[Chorus]
The Dharma body has no form
seek by form and you see only form
seek by sound and you hear only sound
that road will never see the Tathagata
The Dharma body has no form
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Verse 3]
Chanting the Buddha's name out loud
beating the wooden fish hard and high
if you seek through sound alone
only the sound remains

[Pre-Chorus]
If you see me in a form
or seek me in a sound

[Chorus]
The Dharma body has no form
seek by form and you see only form
seek by sound and you hear only sound
that road will never see the Tathagata
The Dharma body has no form
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
where this song comes to an end
in the stillness, the Tathagata
If you see me in a form
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Chorus]
The Dharma body has no form
seek by form and you see only form
seek by sound and you hear only sound
that road will never see the Tathagata
The Dharma body has no form
the very heart that seeks is the Tathagata

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Who sees me by form, who seeks me in sound
walks a wrong path and cannot see the Tathagata
```
