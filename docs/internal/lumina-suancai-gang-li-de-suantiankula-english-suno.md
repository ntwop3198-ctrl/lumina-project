## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Female]
Oh my, snow outside, the cellar is freezing
Dad of my kids, hurry, bring the big stone, press the sauerkraut
[Male]
Coming, coming, oh my back
A lifetime pressing pickle jars, and our life got packed tighter too
[Verse 1]
[Female]
One gust of autumn wind and the ground goes cold
Cartloads of cabbage piled like a hill
Strip the bad leaves, stack them in the jar
Well water icy, sour to the bone
Dad of my kids, you clumsy bear
Lifting a stone and groaning away
You married me on your strength back then
Now one stone and your back gives out
[Male]
Mother of my kids, mind that sharp tongue
I know the bottom of this jar the best
In spring when the old grain ran out
This jar of sauerkraut held up our home
Sauerkraut fried with crispy pork bits
Noodles stewed with pork, the smell hits your nose
Our days were tight, it is true
But I never let you miss a meal
[Pre-Chorus]
[Female]
Oh please, New Year eve you sneaked some pork and hopped from the hot oil
[Male]
What, I was tasting the salt, scouting ahead for my lady
[Female]
Your mouth is harder than a frozen pear
[Male]
My heart is softer than a warm kang
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
Hankies and fans fly to the sky
One big jar, a winter of food
Sour, sweet, bitter, spicy, all inside
Press down the frost, press down the hard times
Get through the winter to the sunny days
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
Full jar, full barn, full of luck and years
Two of us together for a hundred years
[Interlude]
[Verse 2]
[Male]
Winter goes, spring comes, the ice melts
Water at the jar bottom, cabbage flowers float
Corn grit porridge piping hot
Green onions dipped in paste, so good
Now the kids have homes of their own
Our hair has turned white as frost
Dear, you still look like you did back then
And I am still your cart horse
[Female]
The older you get, the sillier you are
Always picking sweet words to fool me
If there is a next life for me
I would still guard this pickle jar with you
Add some wood, warm up the kang
Let us talk all night till the sky turns bright
[Chorus]
[Duet]
Errenzhuan, spinning with joy
Hankies and fans fly to the sky
One big jar, a winter of food
Sour, sweet, bitter, spicy, all inside
Press down the frost, press down the hard times
Get through the winter to the sunny days
Ai hai ai hai ai hai yo
Full jar, full barn, full of luck and years
Two of us together for a hundred years
[Bridge]
[Male]
One stone presses one jar of greens
[Female]
A life of bitter and spicy, we bore it together
[Male]
Not asking for gold or silver
[Female]
Just someone there on the warm kang
[Duet]
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Our good years are in this pickle jar
[Outro]
[Male]
Dear, the sauerkraut pork pot is boiling, let us have a drink
[Female]
A drink it is, old man, fill mine up too
[Duet]
Ha ha ha ha ha
Sour but fragrant
Sweet after the bitter
[Fade Out]
```
