## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
If someone says the Buddha taught
a view of self, a view of others
does that person grasp my meaning
No, World-Honored One, he does not

[Verse 2]
A view of self, a view of others
is not a view, its name is view
one who sets the heart on awakening
should know thus, see thus, trust thus

[Pre-Chorus]
Know, but do not grasp
see, but do not carve it in

[Chorus]
Make no form of Dharma
say you know and you're caught in knowing
say you see and you're caught in seeing
the form called Dharma is not a form
Make no form of Dharma
it is only named the Dharma

[Verse 3]
[Warm and open, gentle acoustic strumming]
I climbed up to the mountaintop
and there a flag was planted
conquered, someone had carved
but the mountain never knew

[Pre-Chorus]
Know, but do not grasp
see, but do not carve it in

[Chorus]
Make no form of Dharma
say you know and you're caught in knowing
say you see and you're caught in seeing
the form called Dharma is not a form
Make no form of Dharma
it is only named the Dharma

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
I pull the flag and start back down
the whole mountain is mine
Know, but do not grasp
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
it is only named the Dharma

[Chorus]
Make no form of Dharma
say you know and you're caught in knowing
say you see and you're caught in seeing
the form called Dharma is not a form
Make no form of Dharma
it is only named the Dharma

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
Toward all dharmas, know it thus, see it thus, trust it thus
and give rise to no form of Dharma
```
