## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
Willow twigs full of spring sap
Tap, tap, coax them gently
Give a little twist and slide it out
That was our whistle

[Verse 2]
Cut it long for a low sound
Cut it short for a high sound
Whose whistle rang the clearest
We sat by the water and tried

[Pre-Chorus]
A sound that came just once in spring
A sound that hardened when the season passed
Back then I did not know
I thought we could blow it any time

[Chorus]
Piriri, piriri
The sound of willow whistles
Carried on the creek
It traveled far away
Piriri, piriri
That spring, by that water

[Verse 3]
If it split, we made another
If it was lost, we snapped a new one
Nothing to be sorry for
The willow was always there

[Bridge]
Is the willow still standing
In that same place today
One twig could blow a whole spring in
I do not blame the hardened season
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Even if it stands, no hand is left to pick it

[Chorus]
Piriri, piriri
The sound of willow whistles
Carried on the creek
It traveled far away
Piriri, piriri
That spring, by that water

[Outro]
Piriri, piriri
```
