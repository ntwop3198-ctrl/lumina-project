## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Water Sounds]
[Distant Children]

[Verse 1]
Out the door after breakfast, home when day was done
The river was our own backyard
Who could swim the farthest, who could hold their breath
That was all it took to fill a day

[Verse 2]
Hand beneath the water, feeling under stones
Something slippery flicks my finger and is gone
Got it, lost it, shouting all the while
And the sun was sinking low

[Chorus]
Burnt black, I walked back home
Till the skin peeled off my back
Back then I thought it was a medal
Arm to arm, who is the darkest one
Where did that summer go

[Verse 3]
Some days I followed Father up the hill
Dig this one, leave that one, he would say
I could not tell the greens from weeds
I just watched the back of his head and walked

[Verse 4]
Find the bellflower by its purple bloom
Know the wild root by its earthy smell
I thought I learned nothing, only followed him
Now I see the following was the lesson

[Chorus]
Burnt black, I walked back home
Till the skin peeled off my back
Back then I thought it was a medal
Arm to arm, who is the darkest one
Where did that summer go

[Bridge]
A little fish beneath a stone
A purple flower led me to the root
Small things always held the way
Peeling skin, I did not run from the sting
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
That summer still lives in me

[Outro]
Now I do not go down to the river
And no one walks the hill with me
[Harmonica Fade Out]
```
