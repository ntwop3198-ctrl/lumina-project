## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
At twelve, they say, he read aloud
The one who sat there gave him a gift
After that he lived through five kings
Through them all he never stepped forward

[Verse 2]
He passed through years when people vanished one by one
He saw those who spoke then vanish too
What he thought in those years was never kept
When a new king said he would take written counsel
That was when he wrote

[Chorus]
He sent up twenty-eight proposals
He wrote the five before, one by one
What was done well, he wrote as well done
What was not, he wrote as not
Of the king just before
He wrote the early years were good, the later not

[Verse 3]
Do not build too many temples, he said
Do not spend on what is empty
Let those who have live as they have
And those who have not, as they have not

[Bridge]
He did not step forward, he waited
When the door opened, he wrote
He set down even the small things of five kings
He did not turn from the shadow of the last
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Into the empty place went twenty-eight proposals

[Chorus]
He sent up twenty-eight proposals
He wrote the five before, one by one
What was done well, he wrote as well done
What was not, he wrote as not
Of the king just before
He wrote the early years were good, the later not

[Verse 4]
The one who took them used them as written
The writing became the order of the land
A few years later he went back to the earth
The writing stayed and went on long

[Outro]
Before him one had sent up ten-some proposals
They were received, and nothing happened
The same kind of writing, one was buried
The other became a country
[End]
```
