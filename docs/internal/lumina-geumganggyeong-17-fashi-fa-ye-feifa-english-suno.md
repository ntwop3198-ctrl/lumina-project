## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Subhuti asks the first question again
where should the mind abide
how should it be tamed
and the Buddha smiled once more

[Verse 2]
Where the Buddha Dipankara dwelled
had I gained a single dharma
you will become a Buddha
I would never have heard those words

[Pre-Chorus]
Because nothing was gained
it was given

[Chorus]
The Dharma is the Dharma, and it is not
all dharmas are the Buddha's Dharma
and all dharmas are not dharmas
so they are named the Dharma
The Dharma is the Dharma, and it is not
where there is no self, all is there

[Verse 3]
Cooking rice and washing dishes
mopping floors and rocking the child to sleep
all of this is Buddha Dharma
and all of it is nothing at all

[Pre-Chorus]
Because nothing was gained
it was given

[Chorus]
The Dharma is the Dharma, and it is not
all dharmas are the Buddha's Dharma
and all dharmas are not dharmas
so they are named the Dharma
The Dharma is the Dharma, and it is not
where there is no self, all is there

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
gaining nothing, he became a Buddha
with no self, the whole world is my home
Because nothing was gained
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
where there is no self, all is there

[Chorus]
The Dharma is the Dharma, and it is not
all dharmas are the Buddha's Dharma
and all dharmas are not dharmas

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
```
