## Style

```
Errenzhuan Northeast China folk song, 124 BPM brisk syncopated bounce, close-miked bamboo clapper transients, comedic male-female duet, quick bantering call-and-response, wide-range lines climbing into top notes with backing voices, lively suona flourishes and bouncy banhu fiddle, Vietnamese vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```
