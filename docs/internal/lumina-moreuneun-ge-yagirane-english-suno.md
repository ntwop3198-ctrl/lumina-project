## Title

```
Not Knowing Is the Best Medicine
```

문서 원본: **모르는 게 약이라네** · 보컬 **남성** — 서커스 무대처럼 과장된 너스레 톤, 짧은 메아리 애드리브

## Style

```
Circus polka with oom-pah tuba, brisk accordion and goofy kazoo slides, brisk bouncy 2/4, theatrical male vocal with playful ad-libs and a short slapback vocal echo, English vocals, short Korean chant in bridge
```

---

## Exclude Styles

```
EDM, trap, rap, hip hop, autotune, heavy metal, sad ballad, dramatic belting, female vocal
```

고급 설정: 기이함 **30%** · 스타일 영향 **70%** · 다양성 보통 · 개인화 끔

## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Tuba]
[Accordion]
[Kazoo]
[Verse 1]
Long ago, a Dutch spectacle maker
Stacked two glass lenses, peering this way and that
In one drop of water, little things wiggling
Good heavens, what a zoo
[Verse 2]
Fresh-baked bread, wiggle wiggle
Creamy white milk, wiggle wiggle
Scratch his beard, wiggle wiggle
Even in his teeth, wiggle wiggle wiggle
[Chorus]
Oh I am starving, nothing I can eat
Everywhere I look, bugs all around
Eyes closed, a feast, eyes open, a jar of bugs
The microscope man goes hungry again today
[Verse 3]
No breakfast, no lunch, just sighs at dinner
His belt goes in one notch a day
His cheeks sink in, skinny as a rail
His belly rumbles like thunder
[Bridge]
[Stop]
His wife had enough and swept the microscope away
Set down a big warm bowl of potato soup
Do not look and there are no bugs, now eat
Swallow down the hungry days too
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Eyes shut tight, slurp slurp, oh my, it is delicious
[Final Chorus]
[Full Band]
Oh I am full, not knowing is the best medicine
See every little thing and you cannot lift a spoon
Look at what to look at, cover what to cover
Close your eyes a bit and life is sweet
[Outro]
[Whispered]
But just one peek at what is in the soup
Oh no, wiggle wiggle again
[Kazoo]
[Tuba]
[Fade Out]

```
