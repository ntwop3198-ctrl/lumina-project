### Title

```
善には善を
```

### Style

```
Cinematic folk pop, acoustic guitar and piano blend, warm grounded male vocal, gentle build to chorus, philosophical yet emotional, talk-singing verses, 78bpm, fade out ending, Japanese only
```

```
female vocal, rap, EDM, heavy drums, electric guitar, autotune, English lyrics, 九霄, 九消
