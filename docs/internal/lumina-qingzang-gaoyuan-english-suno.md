```
[Intro]
[Temple Bells]
[Tibetan Horn]
[Spoken]
The heights of the plateau touch the sky, the roof of the world through all the ages
Chants roll wide across a thousand years, one sacred range rests on the clouds

[Verse 1]
The plateau rises to the highest peaks
White snow summits meet the green ridges
Prayer flags roll on the long far wind
Ancient legends passed from age to age

[Pre-Chorus]
The pure snowland meets the endless sky
A thousand li of highland holds the mist
Faithful steps on the pilgrim road
An upright spirit fills the chest

[Chorus]
Tibetan Plateau, roof of the world
White snow summits touching the sky
Content with the heights, faithful step by step
One line of sacred peaks, year after year

[Verse 2]
Prayer flags at dawn in the morning mist
Yaks stride across the snowfields
Holy mountains and sacred lakes mirror sun and moon
An ancient faith shines on the world

[Pre-Chorus]
The pure snowland meets the endless sky
A thousand li of highland holds the mist
Only a faithful heart travels far
Where the heart rests, the world is bright

[Chorus]
Tibetan Plateau, roof of the world
White snow summits touching the sky
Content with the heights, faithful step by step
One line of sacred peaks, year after year

[Bridge]
Silent prayer flags, the long snowland sky
Keeping my own heart, walking through the snow
Bowing to herd the yaks, honoring the small
Through high cold and thin air, swallowing the toil
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고

[Final Chorus]
Tibetan Plateau, a name that lasts forever
Silent snowfields carry a thousand dreams
Keeping the first heart through the ages
The mighty plateau holding up the sky

Above the highest peaks, the snowland so vast
The snowland plateau, life goes on
```
