## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft piano, low acoustic guitar]

[Verse 1]
Fancy plates are piled high upon the table
A place to feed your pride instead of hunger
A meal full of excess, ornament and vanity
In the end, it's a mirror showing an empty heart

[Verse 2]
When the stomach overflows, the mind grows dull
Focus and judgment slowly start to blur
One who can't master even the hunger of the mouth
Is easily shaken by every other craving in life

[Chorus]
Those who eat with restraint live a life of restraint
Those who eat with greed grow greedy in their lives
The things that look like little habits
Pile up and pile up, till they shake a whole life

[Verse 3]
Facing the empty bowl gone cold
At last I look back on what I truly need to fill
I put away the splendor meant for others' eyes
And quietly gaze upon my small and humble place

[Bridge]
구소구고구기
진공묘유

[Chorus]
```
