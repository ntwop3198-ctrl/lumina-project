[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]
No dazzling words to charm the ear, no sudden twist of fate

The camp he led stood straight and even, as if measured by a rule
Not an inch out of line, he stood upon the ground like stone

Yet that steady stubbornness was the heaviest weapon of all

A heart so straight and clear that gave itself to one command
Shone like a noble jewel through the turmoil of the times
