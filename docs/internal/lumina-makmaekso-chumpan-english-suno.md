## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Verse 1]
[Male]
At a little pub where a Daerim-dong alley ends
Three monks sat down side by side
Jigong with his gourd of wine
Jinmuk who only asks for grain tea
[Verse 2]
[Male]
The last of them is Danxia
Looking around, says the room is cold
Makgeolli, beer and soju mixed
A full glass of makmaekso rolled up
[Chorus]
[Male]
One glass of makmaekso, sway and sway
Three monks dance with their shoulders, sway and sway
Call it wine and they will not drink
Call it grain tea and down it all
[Verse 3]
[Male]
The sleeves of Jinmuk flying
The fan of Jigong spinning round
Danxia with a calm empty face
Just bounces his shoulders to the beat
[Verse 4]
[Male]
The owner runs over in a fright
Monks, this place is a pub, he says
All three shake their heads at once
This right here is the dharma hall
[Bridge]
[Male]
One small glass filled up with laughter
Not turning from the frowning eyes
Setting down rules and setting down face
An alley pub becomes a temple
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
One dance unties an old knot in the heart
[Interlude]
[Final Chorus]
[Male]
[Choir]
One glass of makmaekso, sway and sway
Three monks dance with their shoulders, sway and sway
Lights flashing, speakers thumping
The Daerim-dong alley jumps and shakes
[Outro]
[Male]
[Choir]
Sway and sway
Eolsigu
[Fade Out]

```
