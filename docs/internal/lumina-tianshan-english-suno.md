## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Dombra]
[Dizi]
[Spoken]
Tianshan snow peaks stand in the clouds, crossing the western lands ten thousand li
Camel bells of the Silk Road drift on the wind, one mighty range mirrors the sky

[Verse 1]
Tianshan rises high into the clouds
White snow shines on the great rivers
Camel bells fade on the old Silk Road
Long winds sweep over the mountain pass

[Pre-Chorus]
Green fields join north and south of the range
Evening smoke rests on a thousand li of oases
Herding songs drift under the yurts
Traders raise their whips and ride on

[Chorus]
Tianshan Mountains, ten thousand li of snow
Crossing the west to touch the sky
Content with the vast, standing in the storm
One line of ice peaks, year after year

[Verse 2]
Heavenly Lake mirrors the ice mountains
Herding songs cross endless grasslands
Caravans break the silence of the sand
A bold spirit runs through a thousand years

[Pre-Chorus]
Green fields join north and south of the range
The ancient road has turned to rolling waves
Only the steadfast travel far
A wide spirit lives on in the heart

[Chorus]
Tianshan Mountains, ten thousand li of snow
Crossing the west to touch the sky
Content with the vast, standing in the storm
One line of ice peaks, year after year

[Bridge]
Snow on Heavenly Lake, one range to the sky
Camel bells go far on their own, leaning on no one
Bowing to tend the flock, honoring the small
On the long Gobi road, swallowing the toil
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Ten thousand li of snow peaks, life goes on

[Final Chorus]
Tianshan Mountains, a name that lasts forever
Silent snow peaks hold a thousand dreams
Keeping the first heart through the ages
Mighty mountains holding up the sky

[Outro]
On the Tianshan heights, by Heavenly Lake
Ten thousand li of snow peaks, life goes on
[Fade Out]
```
