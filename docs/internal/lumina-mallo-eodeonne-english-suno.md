## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
An army came down from the north
Some said give up land without a fight
Up high, it was being settled that way
This man said no

[Verse 2]
He crossed alone and took a seat
They told him to bow
Unless we sit face to face as equals
He said, I will not begin to speak

[Chorus]
He won it with words
Won it without raising an army
He saw it was not the land they wanted
Once he saw, he said he would give them that
Then said the road between was blocked
To clear it, the land between must be ours

[Verse 3]
They took his words
Turned their army and withdrew
No battle, no one faded
And the land had grown instead

[Bridge]
No army with him, he crossed and sat alone
He would not bow, he waited for an equal seat
In one word he saw what they wanted
He faced it alone and did not turn away
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
He went empty-handed and came back with six towns

[Chorus]
He won it with words
Won it without raising an army
He saw it was not the land they wanted
Once he saw, he said he would give them that
Then said the road between was blocked
To clear it, the land between must be ours

[Verse 4]
He came back and built walls
On the new land he raised six forts
Twice more those forts were needed
What he built held them then

[Outro]
Won by words, it looked so light
Yet the land remained for long
Words have no shape at all
Yet from those words came six forts
[End]
```
