## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Soft moktak and nylon guitar]

[Verse 1]
Grind the whole world into dust
would that dust be a great deal
A great deal, World-Honored One
the dust is not dust, its name is dust

[Verse 2]
The world the Tathagata speaks of
is not a world, its name is world
if the world truly existed
it would be one single whole

[Pre-Chorus]
Even to call it one whole
words cannot say

[Chorus]
The world is not the world
gather the dust and call it world
break the world and call it dust
only the name has changed
The world is not the world
yet ordinary people live clinging to it

[Verse 3]
[Warm and reflective, gentle acoustic strum]
On moving day we carried everything out
and only empty rooms remained
where did our home go
did it ride off with the boxes

[Pre-Chorus]
Even to call it one whole
words cannot say

[Chorus]
The world is not the world
gather the dust and call it world
break the world and call it dust
only the name has changed
The world is not the world
yet ordinary people live clinging to it

[Bridge]
[Guitar drops, daegeum only]
everything out, an empty room
wherever we unpack is home again
Even to call it one whole
진공묘유
Seek the simple, face the hard, seek within
yet ordinary people live clinging to it

[Chorus]
The world is not the world
gather the dust and call it world
break the world and call it dust
only the name has changed
The world is not the world
yet ordinary people live clinging to it

[Outro]
[Chant, slow and low with fading moktak]
What is called one whole cannot be spoken
only ordinary people cling to it
```
