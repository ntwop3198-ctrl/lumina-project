## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Guzheng]
[Pipa]
[Spoken]
Water shimmering under clear skies, misty hills and willows like smoke
Melting snow on Broken Bridge, a thousand-year dream, a lake of misty rain whispering love

[Verse 1]
The ten scenes of West Lake come into a painting
Spring dawn on Su Causeway, willow winds open
The shadow of Leifeng Pagoda in the setting sun
Ancient legends linger with the dust

[Pre-Chorus]
Ink-wash Jiangnan, spring is warm
Poets and painters wrote their verses
Painted boats rock and the light ripples
One clear tune brushes the heartstrings

[Chorus]
West Lake, a scroll of misty rain
Spring dawn on Su Causeway touching the sky
Content in quiet, hidden in the mist
One small boat, year after year

[Verse 2]
Melting snow on Broken Bridge holds deep love
Three Pools Mirroring the Moon light the heart of the water
The evening bell at Nanping rings far
A gentle ink scent carried to this day

[Pre-Chorus]
Ink-wash Jiangnan, spring is warm
A thousand years of grace become long waves
Still water runs deep and travels far
My heart walks slowly with the blue water

[Chorus]
West Lake, a scroll of misty rain
Spring dawn on Su Causeway touching the sky
Content in quiet, hidden in the mist
One small boat, year after year

[Bridge]
The lake shines in silence, misty rain like a dream
Keeping a clear heart, never bending
Bowing at the oar, honoring the small
Through wind and rain, swallowing the toil
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
A scroll of misty rain, ink scent flowing

[Final Chorus]
West Lake, a name that lasts forever
Silent blue water holds a thousand dreams
Passed down for ages, keeping the first heart
Misty waves mirror the sky

[Outro]
Walking Su Causeway, by Broken Bridge
A scroll of misty rain, ink scent flowing
Ink-wash Jiangnan, fragrant for a thousand years
[Fade Out]
```
