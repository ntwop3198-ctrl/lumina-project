## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Temple Block]
[Guitar Shuffle]
One monk carries his own water
[Verse 1]
A monk lived alone in a mountain temple
When thirsty, he went down by himself
And hauled up two buckets on a pole
Nobody told him to
If he did not, he would be thirsty
So he just did
And in that temple
The water jar was always full
[Pre-Chorus]
One day another monk arrived
[Chorus]
With two monks now
They carried one bucket together
One in front, one behind
Who has more weight, who has less
Bickering all the way
But the water came up
With two of them
It still worked out
[Verse 2]
Then a third one came
Now there were three
The first one thought
The other two will go
The second one thought
The new guy should go
The third one thought
I just got here
[Pre-Chorus]
The three just stared at each other
Till the sun went down
[Chorus]
With three monks now
Nobody fetched the water
The jar ran dry
Throats burned with thirst
More people
Less water
Thought three would make it easier
But they only got thirstier
[Bridge]
[Guitar Only]
[Spoken]
Groups are like that too
You think more hands make work light
But all just sit and wait for someone else
Then you quietly pick up the pole
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
Someone just has to stand up first
[Final Chorus]
Three monks
One of them stood up
Without a word, took the pole
And went down the mountain
When he came back
The other two had followed
When one picks it up first
The rest pick up too
[Outro]
[Temple Block]
Three monks, no water to drink
But if one goes first
There is water after all
[Temple Bell]
[Fade Out]

```
