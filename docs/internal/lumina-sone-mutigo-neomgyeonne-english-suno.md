## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
He was the fifth son
The only brother to pass the exam
When his father rose, he went out front
The one blocking the road, he cleared away too

[Verse 2]
When the country stood, he was left off the honors
The seat of the heir went to the youngest
That is how his father chose
And a few years passed

[Chorus]
Hands stained, then handed on
At night he led soldiers in
Two younger brothers faded that day
The man who raised his father faded that day too
No one told him to
He decided, and he did it himself

[Verse 3]
He did not sit at once
He set his brother on the seat for a few years
Meanwhile another brother raised soldiers
He struck that brother too, and only then he sat

[Bridge]
He struck his brothers, struck the house of his wife
Cleared even the road behind his son
He saw the smallest gap before anyone
He did not shy from staining his own hands

He took back the private soldiers of every house
He removed the family of his wife
```
