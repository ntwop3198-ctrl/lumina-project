## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
The alarm keeps ringing right on time
My head keeps nodding as I doze
When people talk I nod along
I'm a master at pretending that I know

[Pre-Chorus]
I sat down at the desk a while ago
But my fingers just keep rummaging the drawer

[Chorus]
In and out, back and forth
Oh well, the day's already gone
Can't say I really got a thing done
So how come I'm this worn out

[Verse 2]
I open up the fridge, then close it again
Stare blankly at the same side dish I just saw
Should I step out for some air, I go back and forth
My shoes go on and off all by themselves

[Bridge]
구소구고구기
Taking less and enduring, I quietly face myself
진공묘유
Even in an empty day with nothing filled, I was here

[Final Chorus]
I ate my meals right on time
I nodded off and got my sleep
I nodded along and saw some friends
So hey, I'd say that's living

[Outro]
Back and forth
Wobble and sway
Still, this day of mine
Sure went by just fine
```
