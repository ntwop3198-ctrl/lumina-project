## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro: erhu and sanxian]

[Verse 1]
When I was young I had so many friends
One table couldn't hold us so we set up two
Everyone was brother everyone was kin
Now I can't even recall their names

[Verse 2]
The ones who borrowed money went far away
The ones who lied put on another face
The year I fell I sat and counted
Just one pair of chopsticks left at the table

[Pre-Chorus]
It's not that I was all that choosy
Life did the choosing for me

[Chorus]
The one who stayed
Didn't lecture didn't laugh didn't hide
When I hit bottom he sat right here
When I rose again he's still sitting the same
The one who stayed
Doesn't say much can't talk it pretty
But whenever I look back
He's always there

[Verse 3]
Some went off to gild the lily for the lucky
Some turned their backs and knew me not
I don't hold any of it against them
Everyone's got their own hard road

[Bridge: instruments drop to harmonica and voice]
Nine of them left and they're gone
I can't even say which nine they were
But the face of this one
I could draw it with my eyes closed

[Final Chorus: erhu returns, fuller]
The one who stayed
Didn't lecture didn't laugh didn't hide
When I hit bottom he sat right here
When I rose again he's still sitting the same
The one who stayed
Doesn't say much can't talk it pretty
But whenever I look back
He's always there

[Outro: erhu fading]
One is enough
Truly enough
```
