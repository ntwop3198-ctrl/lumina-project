## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro]
[Verse 1]
Holding a pair of twins in her arms
A young mother walks a country path
She meets two men with mischief in mind
And one of them a village schoolmaster
Out to trip her up, a trap inside the words
Of these two babies, which is the master, which is the lad
[Pre-Chorus]
Before he finished, her eyes made a turn
However deep the trick, it could not slip past her ears
Calm and steady, a little smile at her lips
She already knew how to answer back
[Chorus]
The master is my son, the lad is my son too
One line turned two rascals into her sons
The crowd around burst out laughing
Wit has won, rudeness lost it all
[Verse 2]
Their faces flushed red, then went pale
Mouths hanging open, not a word to say
The neighbors clapped and cheered
She held her babies, turned and walked away
[Bridge]
No rush, no panic, that is real skill
One small reply caught the trap
Faced the shame head on, never turned aside
Soft beats hard, she answered with a smile
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
True wisdom hides inside the words
[Chorus]
The master is my son, the lad is my son too
One line turned two rascals into her sons
The crowd around burst out laughing
Wit has won, rudeness lost it all
[Outro]
This tale is told year after year
Teaching us to meet a taunt with ease
True wisdom never rushes
One line, and four ounces move a thousand pounds
[Fade Out]

```
