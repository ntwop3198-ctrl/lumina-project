## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Intro: piano alone, long silences]

[Verse 1]
That year when you asked me I just bowed my head and said nothing
Not that there was nothing in my heart I just couldn't get it out
You took my silence as me not caring
The truth is I was afraid that if I spoke it would mean goodbye

[Verse 2]
I can't write love poems don't know a thing about romance
I just held my wages tight and pressed them in your hand for rice
You said I was like a block of wood I smiled and didn't explain
Even wood can feel the pain it just can't make a sound

[Pre-Chorus]
The words you wanted I practiced a thousand times
But every time they reached my lips they turned into the weather

[Chorus]
I truly loved you
I just said it far too late
The day you left I stood behind the door
One sentence stuck in my throat never came out all my life
I truly loved you
Not that I wouldn't but I wasn't worthy
The promise that you wanted it's not that I didn't want to give
I was afraid I'd give it and not be able to keep it

[Verse 3]
Now I've learned how to say it but no one asks anymore
All the words are in this room I say them to an empty chair
You asked me which line of your poem I was
The truth is all my life I only ever wrote one line of you

[Bridge: instruments drop to piano and voice]
From telling each other everything to having nothing left to say
What stood between wasn't a lack of love
I thought the days were still long
I thought you'd ask me one more time

[Final Chorus: cello and strings enter, fuller]
I truly loved you
I just said it far too late
The day you left I stood behind the door
One sentence stuck in my throat never came out all my life
I truly loved you
Not that I wouldn't but I wasn't worthy
The promise that you wanted it's not that I didn't want to give
I was afraid I'd give it and not be able to keep it

[Outro: piano fade]
If you'd only ask me once more
This time I'd answer you
```
