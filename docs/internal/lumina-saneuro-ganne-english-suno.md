## Lyrics (v1)

```
[Verse 1]
He was the son of a king
Next in line to take the throne
There was just nothing left to take
How old he was, no one wrote down

[Verse 2]
When they spoke of giving it away
He alone said no
What had lasted a thousand years
Could not be handed over in a day

[Chorus]
He went to the mountains
Left those who tried to stop him
They say he wore rough clothes
They say he ate roots of grass
He did not rise up to fight
And he did not bow to follow

[Verse 3]
His father gave it up and took a high seat
His sister married into the other side
The whole house went that way
Only he walked the other way

[Bridge]
He left all he was owed and went up the hills
The high seat stayed down below
In rough cloth he saw what is real
Cold and alone, he did not turn away
구소구고구기, 밖에서 찾지 않고
He left nothing, so only a name remains

[Chorus]
He went to the mountains
Left those who tried to stop him
They say he wore rough clothes
They say he ate roots of grass
He did not rise up to fight
And he did not bow to follow

[Verse 4]
When he faded, no one wrote down
Where he was buried, no one wrote down
Some say he went farther north
Some say he never left that mountain

[Outro]
All that remained was the name of his clothes
By those clothes the people called him
His face and name grew faint
Only the clothes he wore have lasted a thousand years
[End]
```
